# Tamar Vey v699-v6 (2) Method Flow overlay

Keep Method Flow and all failure evidence outside the compact handoff baton. Preserve the planning, x1, x2, final, canonical, and route observations as separate stages. A rejected mutation is a retained negative and a successful guard witness; it is not a failed method unless the evaluator or workflow itself failed.

Every parser fault, output overflow, wrong-path assumption, dirty-state refusal, test failure, workaround, and bounded recovery remains paired. Recovery does not erase, reclassify, or retroactively promote its failed witness. Inherited counters are folded once, and external post-seal overlays remain distinct from repository-sealed counts.

The exact-final canonical aggregate may be invoked at most once after a clean pushed final. A success is never replayed. A failed canonical has zero success credit; any narrowly justified component recovery is separately named and cannot promote the failed invocation.
