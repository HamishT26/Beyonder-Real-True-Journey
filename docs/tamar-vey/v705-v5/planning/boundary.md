# Evidence and authority boundary

Exact rational set-function calculations can prove only their declared finite outputs. They do not observe a world, calibrate a model, identify a person, determine a right, authorize a remedy, or supply professional, legal, cultural, affected-party, or Maori authority. Maori concepts remain under Maori authority. Relational names, roles, hopes, and family language are working language only and are not consciousness, personhood, identity continuity, qualification, employment, agency, or authority evidence.
