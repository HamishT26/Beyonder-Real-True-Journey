# v642-v2 primary and official source ledger

The machine-readable ledger contains 38 sources: 20 `current`, 14 `stable`, 3 `draft`, and 1 `watch`. Stable or current official publications anchor the phase. Draft and watch sources remain visibly provisional and cannot silently replace stable pins.

Four v642-v2 additions extend the inherited 34-source ledger:

- W3C RDF Dataset Canonicalization 1.0, Recommendation of 21 May 2024, for graph canonicalization and parity boundaries.
- BIPM, *The International System of Units*, 9th edition updated in 2026, for dimensions and unit typing.
- ICH E9(R1), final Step 4 addendum adopted 20 November 2019, for estimands, intercurrent events, and sensitivity discipline.
- RFC 8785, informational JSON Canonicalization Scheme, for deterministic JSON and strict number-domain boundaries.

The ledger records provenance and version status. It does not validate GMUT, THOS superiority, Freed ID production, legal or cultural authority, Māori authority, deployment, security completeness, accessibility completeness, or independent reproduction.
