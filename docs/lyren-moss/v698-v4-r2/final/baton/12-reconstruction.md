# Bounded reconstruction protocol

A successor can reconstruct the repository evidence without executing Lyren's domain engine. First, resolve the exact Lyren branch and exact final supplied by the external terminal receipt. Confirm that local, upstream, tracking and a fresh live remote all identify that same commit, with typed zero-ahead and zero-behind divergence and a clean checkout. Confirm that the final has x2 as its single parent, x2 has x1 in its ancestry, x1 has the planning checkpoint in its ancestry, and there are three post-planning commits with no merge. These checks establish repository lifecycle shape only; they do not reproduce the mathematical behavior independently.

Second, read the phase truth, proposal outcomes, gate register, retained-negative index, Method Flow index, lifecycle, checklist, route candidate and handoff baton from exact Git blobs. Parse every phase JSON document strictly. Replay the final manifest, owner manifest and content seal against exact Git-blob bytes and SHA-256 values; do not substitute checkout bytes on Windows because line-ending conversion is a distinct byte domain. Confirm 600 unique proposal identifiers, exactly 510 completed, 30 represented, 30 open_gap and 30 exact_gate labels, 1,200 retained domain subjects plus every named operational failure, and no unknown outcome label. Confirm that source totals are folded exactly once and that the arithmetic for methods, passing witnesses, failed witnesses, total witnesses, negatives, gaps and gates is internally consistent.

Third, inspect x1 and x2 session summaries rather than rerunning them during canonical validation. Each session must report 300 pristine comparisons, 300 malformed candidate subjects, 300 detached clone subjects, 600 separate passing refusals and 300 CFR restorations with zero filesystem deletions. The fifteen additional tests and five scene models are already immutable x2 evidence; canonical validation checks their metadata and hashes only. Any later rerun is a new attributable observation, not a retroactive replacement.

Fourth, apply bounded privacy and path hygiene to the exact owner delta. A clear five-class pattern scan is only a scoped observation. It does not establish complete privacy, complete accessibility or exhaustive security. Never persist native task handles, app state, raw transcripts, credentials, hidden reasoning or private absolute paths in a handoff. Preserve the missing local induction-criteria reference as a route-preflight gap and rely on Hamish's newest live instruction plus the current roster and authority state rather than inventing a substitute rule.

Finally, keep canonical, route and delivery states separate. One successful exact-final metadata canonical may be invoked after the pushed equality gate and must not be replayed. Only after that success may the exact-title Talen Briar task be uniquely resolved and immediately reread. A single accepted, opaque or unresolved submission closes the send latch; it does not prove recipient completion and never licenses a second confirmation or monitoring pass. This protocol supports inspectability and reversibility while preserving every protected claim and the terminal verdict NOT_READY_FOR_STAGE_20.
