# Hazard referral

1. Confirm that the alarm, location, worker, measurement, and response are fictional.
2. Run `python -X utf8 scripts/ghc_family_hazard_referral.py --output <owner-receipt.json>`.
3. Require one valid structural fixture and five rejected mutations.
4. Preserve the hazard hold and refer every real confined-space or workplace decision to competent people and authorities.

Never treat the result as safety advice, emergency direction, professional approval, production evidence, or Stage 20 readiness.
