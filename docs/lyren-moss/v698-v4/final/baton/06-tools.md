# Skills, runners and practices

Twenty phase-local skills, ten paired text runners and five curated local packages were built, smoke-tested and indexed without modifying shared or sibling lanes. The eight bounded practice lenses were:

- hypergraph theorist: Define finite directed-hypergraph semantics and ambiguity limits.
- graph algorithm engineer: Implement deterministic traversal, projection and component checks.
- formal methods engineer: Keep request closure, falsifiers and refusal envelopes exact.
- provenance systems designer: Bind every result to its immutable synthetic request.
- software verification engineer: Exercise bounded positive, negative and metamorphic checks.
- accessible information designer: Represent scenes and tables without claiming complete accessibility.
- archival metadata analyst: Preserve source, correction and handoff lineage.
- rights and governance boundary analyst: Keep authority-dependent decisions gated.

These are analytical lenses, not claims that Lyren is professionally qualified or authorized in those occupations. Four successor recommendations remain suggestions only: dependency-risk analysis, explainable visualization evaluation, incident provenance review and discrete-optimization testing.
