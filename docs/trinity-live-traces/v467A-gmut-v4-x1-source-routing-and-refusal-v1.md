# v467A GMUT v4 x1 Source Routing And Refusal

Status: SOURCE_ROUTING_AND_REFUSAL_ACTIVE

Prepared: 2026-06-01T21:02:38+12:00

The v4 x1 blocker ledger needs a strict source-routing layer because source existence is not the same as source authority, and source authority is not the same as GMUT validation.

## Allowed Source Routes

`exact_local_artifact_available` may support row identity and exact local artifact binding only.

`durable_repo` may support artifact existence when directly referenced.

`v13_canon_reference_only` may support lineage, not current closure by itself.

`official_primary` and `peer_reviewed` may support external context and constraints only.

`external_context_only` may frame background, not prove GMUT.

`advisory_only` may shape schema and lint language, not evidence.

`journey_context_not_canon` may preserve terminology history only with local path/line references.

`blocker` and `open_gap` carry missing or insufficient evidence forward.

## Refusal Lints

The active refusal lints are overclaiming validation or closure, execution leakage, literal/derived mixing, conflict suppression, confidence inflation, missing endcap, forbidden inference, route break, unsupported authority, comparison leakage, no-result synonym leakage, and Journey/Solas canon leakage.

## Blocked Claims

The layer blocks GMUT validation, null recovery, baseline recovery, dimensional/SI closure, conservation/exchange closure, fifth-force/equivalence safety, consciousness measurement bridge closure, spiritual proof, final physics, canon promotion, and fixture execution.

## Decision

Any claim not routed to an allowed source class is refused. Any claim requiring execution is blocked. Any Journey/Solas claim outside `journey_context_not_canon` is refused.
