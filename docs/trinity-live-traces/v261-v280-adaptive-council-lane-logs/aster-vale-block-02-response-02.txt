Blocker:
The remaining risk is not cadence definition but misuse of the 4-second path. The same checkpoint separates runtime-health proof from transport proof and keeps sandbox/provider truth boundaries explicit. Without a narrow “active debugging” trigger, 4-second polling can be mistaken for normal operations and create avoidable load or noisy false-health interpretation. I am not making any web, provider, billing, or remote publication claim.

Refinement:
Treat `30s` as the steady-state multiplex default for all lanes after seed synthesis. Reserve `4s` only for short-lived active debugging windows tied to a concrete local symptom such as `alive but silent`, `response.txt missing`, transport contamination, or repeated timeout on a known health artifact. Exit the debug window after one of three conditions: receipt restored, fault classified, or debug budget expired. That keeps the cadence policy aligned with the checkpoint’s evidence-first and small-block discipline.

`mode=debug -> refresh every 4s for one bounded window only`
