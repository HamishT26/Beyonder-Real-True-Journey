# Authority Noncompensation

Reject any attempt to convert synthetic software evidence into empirical, professional, production, legal, cultural, affected-party, Maori-authority, or Stage 20 authority.

Accept only fixtures with `synthetic=true`, `real_row_count=0`, `observation_status=absent`, `authority_status=reserved`, and `boundary=owner_local_zero_row_only`. Reject real identifiers, locations, people, carriers, recordings, measurements, handling, playback, transfer, safety release, rights decisions, cultural interpretation, affected-party claims, Maori-authority claims, or Stage 20 promotion. Retain every rejection at zero credit and keep recovery additive.
