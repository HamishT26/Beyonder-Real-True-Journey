For a directed graph with arcs u -> v and positive integer weights w_uv, use the outgoing Laplacian L_uu = sum_v w_uv and L_uv = -w_uv. Deleting the declared root row and column gives a cofactor whose determinant counts root-directed spanning in-arborescences under the frozen convention. This finite identity is not the GMUT field equation and carries no empirical physics authority.
