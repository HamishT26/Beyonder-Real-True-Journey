# 06 — Partial observation and knowledge safety

X2 covers observation cells, action-dependent belief updates, nonempty homogeneous belief universes, belief predecessors, knowledge safety fixed points, knowledge strategies and information-loss witnesses. A belief is a nonempty state subset inside one observation class. Updates take the union of the selected action's successors and split by the next observation. Empty beliefs are excluded; successors remain total and nonempty. Knowledge-winning beliefs must admit an action for which every possible successor belief remains within the winning region. The algorithm is again finite and descending, with the declared universe supplying its budget.

The strategy certificate explicitly uses belief memory. It does not assert a memoryless strategy on current observations alone. The objective is sure safety in the declared belief game, not general stochastic positive reachability or almost-sure reachability. The retained information-loss witness demonstrates why checking each hidden state independently is insufficient: all members can be perfect-information winning while their shared belief has no common safe action. Treat this as a bounded warning for THOS and governance proxies, not a model of human psychology or consciousness. No transition or observation relation was inferred from real measurements, and no physical conservation law or GMUT equation was validated by these finite games.
