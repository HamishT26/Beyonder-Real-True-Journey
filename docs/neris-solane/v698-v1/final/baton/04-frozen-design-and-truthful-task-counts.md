# 04 — Frozen design and truthful task counts

The program declares 600 new operation/input contracts sharing thirty profiles across twenty mechanisms. This is not 600 independent algorithms, experiments, devices or systems. Three hundred complete inherited Elaren definitions and stored observations were selected for zero-credit integrity learning. Source intake read exact Git blobs and checked stored manifests without running any source engine, canonical, test, smoke or model. The accessible title comparison found no exact collision in its declared corpus, but universal novelty across all historical proposals is not claimed. The definitions and complete expected envelopes were frozen before either production tranche.

Each session contains 300 accepting safe contracts, 300 invalid-horizon candidate subjects and 300 annotated detached-clone subjects. Every candidate and clone remains an invalid subject with zero original completion credit. Their correctly passing refusals are separate witnesses. Three hundred complete definition reviews and detached in-memory restorations are the session's CFR work; they delete no filesystem object and do not stand in for real host cleanup. Exact-approval and blocked portfolios contain fifty and thirty held records respectively; no missing prerequisite was invented to execute them. Caps are ceilings, never permission to fabricate unsafe actions or expand authority to meet counts.
