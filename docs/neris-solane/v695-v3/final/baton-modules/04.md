# 04 — Formal model and cross-formalism lesson

Let the finite alphabet be Sigma and the independence relation I be symmetric and irreflexive. Dependence is the complement of I, so each symbol depends on itself. A trace congruence is generated by swapping adjacent independent symbols. The engine preserves occurrences by index, which matters when a label repeats. The dependency DAG relates earlier dependent occurrences; its transitive closure gives the occurrence-poset order. Topological linearizations supply the finite trace class under an explicit cap.

The five fixtures are commute, diamond, pipeline, mutex and weave. They were chosen to expose repeated labels, a fork-join shape, overlapping independence pairs, a separator action and a woven partial order. These are not real workloads. Their names do not imply safety, fairness, cost, time or human meaning. Exact finite combinatorics removes one source of floating-point uncertainty, but it does not measure model adequacy.

Rowan's Petri tools were invoked five times on one explicit synthetic fork-join net: structure, firing, a supplied invariant, bounded reachability and an interpretation boundary. Those calls were a comparison aid with zero novelty credit. A Petri marking graph and a trace monoid can describe related concurrency aspects, but neither representation automatically refines the other. A valid refinement needs a declared map, a preserved property and counterexamples.

The TeX file states the finite algebraic definition and was not rendered. Nothing in this work supplies a justified new tensor in the Grand Mandala equation, a likelihood, a physical unit or a detected field. In particular, vocabulary such as event, flow, invariant or energy cannot establish a coupling to Omega_AB without a typed physical model and observations.
