# Module 07 - Interpretation, assumptions, and three-pillar relevance

The phase deliberately separates an algebraic identity, a discrete approximation, a numerical stability claim, and an empirical physical claim. These layers can support one another, but none can be substituted silently for another. A zero residual says that a supplied vector satisfies one supplied finite equation. It does not establish consistency order, convergence, continuum adequacy, measured agreement, or physical truth.

Uniform-grid and cell-width operations make coordinate ordering explicit. Linear interpolation is exact for an affine function inside its bracket. Forward and backward differences attach results to different node alignments. A central difference reproduces the derivative of a quadratic at interior nodes, and a centered second difference reproduces its constant second derivative. These are polynomial-exactness facts under the declared stencil, not a proof that arbitrary data arise from a differentiable function.

The trapezoid rule is exact for linear sequences. The L1 grid error names its pointwise alignment and denominator. The observed-order helper deliberately accepts only an exact small integer order under a declared refinement ratio. Three convenient error levels cannot establish that a numerical method has entered an asymptotic regime. Real convergence evidence needs a specified problem, exact or accepted reference solution, nested grids, a norm, refinement ratios, boundary treatment, roundoff analysis, and enough levels to challenge the proposed rate.

The Poisson solver handles a small constant-forcing one-dimensional Dirichlet system with rational arithmetic. The manufactured solution gives zero residual because it was constructed for that equation. This is useful implementation evidence, yet the inverse crime remains visible: using the same discrete relation to create and verify an answer can hide modelling error. A future empirical or independent study must bring a separately justified reference and an accepted reviewer.

Flux balance telescopes exactly for the finite sequence because adjacent internal terms cancel. That algebra can be a valuable conservation check. It becomes a physical conservation statement only after variables, units, control volumes, boundary orientation, source terms, calibration, uncertainty, and observation procedures are supplied. The current payloads have none of those empirical bindings.

Richardson extrapolation cancels one declared leading error term. Its exact arithmetic does not prove that two estimates share the assumed asymptotic expansion. Adaptive splitting bisects intervals whose supplied indicator exceeds a threshold. The indicator is not automatically a rigorous error bound. A useful next step would compare an estimator with observed error on a governed manufactured problem and retain cases where refinement does not improve the chosen norm.

The CFL helper calculates one dimensionless ratio and labels whether it meets a selected bound. The bound is tied to a scheme and domain; it is not a universal stability law. The heat-step helper refuses alpha above one half. An external exact counterexample explains why the restriction matters: starting from `[0,1,0,1,0]`, an unrestricted alpha of one gives `[0,-1,2,-1,0]` and raises the selected energy from 2 to 10. Alpha one quarter gives `[0,1/2,1/2,1/2,0]` and lowers the same energy to one quarter.

That counterexample rejects an unrestricted numerical energy-decay claim. It does not challenge the physical second law of thermodynamics. The finite state, artificial time step, norm, boundary rule, and dimensionless values are mathematical. Calling the quantity energy does not create physical units or a measurement. A candidate thermo-psyche principle must likewise state its state variables, dynamics, sign domain, coarse-graining, observable map, uncertainty, and falsifier before monotonicity can be assessed.

Findiff compared ten polynomial derivative cases, Numdifftools compared ten adaptive derivative cases, and meshio round-tripped ten synthetic line meshes on D. All thirty comparisons passed their declared tolerances or structural equality checks. They remain same-owner comparisons in one package closure. Different libraries and file formats do not become independent reproduction merely by having different code paths.

For GMUT Mind, this phase recommends an observable contract for every proposed term: quantity, units, allowed domain, calibration, uncertainty, competing model, disconfirming result, and data-governance conditions. Internal consistency can reject a malformed formulation but cannot confirm a universal theory. For THOS Body, typed refusal, exact rational cases, resource ceilings, and immutable receipts make a small computation easier to audit and correct. For Freed ID and CBR Heart, provenance binding and claim reservation prevent possession of bytes or a matching digest from being mistaken for consent, rights, identity, or authority.

No new fundamental law, unsolved mathematical problem solution, empirical GMUT confirmation, Theory-of-Everything proof, AGI or ASI evidence, production THOS certification, real Freed ID lifecycle, consciousness claim, or Stage 20 authority is established. The constructive result is a clearer set of bounded tests and missing prerequisites.
