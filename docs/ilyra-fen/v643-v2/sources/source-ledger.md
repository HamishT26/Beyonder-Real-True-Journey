The effective ledger inherits 88 v643-v1 sources without changing their status and adds 12 phase-local primary or official sources, for 100 total: 41 `current`, 51 `stable`, six `draft`, and two `watch`.

The new set covers scalar-tensor radiative stability, frame-invariant observables, EFT matching and running, ICH estimands, CONSORT 2025, NIST digital-identity recovery, UN disability and procedural-accommodation authority, Te Mana Raraunga Māori Data Sovereignty Principles, W3C Privacy Principles, WCAG 2.2, the CSS Color Adjustment draft, and Crooks' fluctuation theorem.

Statuses are not flattened. A citation supplies vocabulary, constraints, and provenance; it does not supply real GMUT measurements, THOS participants, production keys or services, affected-party acceptance, Māori authority, legal interpretation, cultural ratification, qualified accessibility assessment, exhaustive privacy or security, or independent-team reproduction.
