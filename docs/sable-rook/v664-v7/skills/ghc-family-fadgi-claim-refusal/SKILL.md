# GHC Family FADGI Claim Refusal

Use this skill when a packet cites FADGI performance levels, metrics, targets, formats, or measurement parameters.

Confirm the cited source and declared applicability, then require zero readings, zero score, zero product identity, and explicit refusal of conformance, certification, endorsement, and preservation-result claims. Reject any fixture that turns a threshold or citation into an observed pass.

Passing means the refusal boundary works on the supplied synthetic fixture. It provides no measurement, laboratory result, professional review, product assessment, or institutional authority.
