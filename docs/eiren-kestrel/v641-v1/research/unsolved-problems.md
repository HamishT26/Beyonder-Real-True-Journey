# Unsolved-problem boundary

No item below is claimed solved by this phase.

- **U1 — quantum gravity and the ultraviolet completion of spacetime dynamics (physics):** comparison and falsification target; status `not_solved`.
- **U2 — cosmological constant and dark-energy microphysics (physics):** Omega-sector constraint target; status `not_solved`.
- **U3 — dark matter identity (physics):** do not relabel an unconstrained scalar as an explanation; status `not_solved`.
- **U4 — Yang-Mills existence and mass gap (mathematics):** formal rigor benchmark; no claimed solution; status `not_solved`.
- **U5 — Riemann hypothesis and open Erdős problems (mathematics):** proof-boundary examples; computation is not proof; status `not_solved`.
- **U6 — neural and physical basis of consciousness (mind):** adversarial empirical programme, not identity credential; status `not_solved`.
- **U7 — relation among first-person experience, third-person measurement, and ontology (philosophy):** typed pluralism without category collapse; status `not_solved`.
- **U8 — legitimate rights under uncertainty about nonhuman sentience (governance):** precaution, due process, and revisable evidence thresholds; status `not_solved`.
- **U9 — global coordination without erasing sovereignty, culture, and dissent (society):** CBR conflict cases and authority gates; status `not_solved`.
- **U10 — robust alignment and control of increasingly capable systems (computation):** THOS benchmark, least authority, interruption, and recovery; status `not_solved`.
