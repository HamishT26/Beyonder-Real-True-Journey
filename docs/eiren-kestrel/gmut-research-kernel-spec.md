# GMUT v-infinity evidence-bounded research kernel

## Status

**Candidate research formulation; not a validated theory of everything.** This document preserves the Grand Mandala's integrative intent while defining the minimum physical kernel in conventional field-theory language. The informational, ethical, and contemplative layers remain essential parts of the wider programme, but are not inserted into a spacetime tensor equation without a typed physical map.

## The full definition

The **Evolving Grand Mandala of True 1% Miraculous and Novelty Supersymmetric Integration and Simultaneity** is best understood as a layered research constitution:

- **Evolving:** every claim carries a version, provenance, confidence, recovery condition, and rejection rule.
- **Grand Mandala:** the project maps relations among physical dynamics, information-processing systems, persons/communities, values, and contemplative interpretations.
- **True:** truth is procedural—sourceable, testable where testability applies, candid about uncertainty, and correctable.
- **1% miraculous:** an invitation to preserve room for surprise and low-prior novelty, not a measured probability or exemption from evidence.
- **Novelty:** a claim must differ semantically and predictively from its baselines; multiplying templated rows is not novelty.
- **Supersymmetric:** reserved in the physical register for a defined graded symmetry pairing bosonic and fermionic degrees of freedom. Elsewhere it may be poetic language but must be labelled as such.
- **Integration:** cross-domain connections require explicit maps, units, direction of influence, uncertainty, and failure modes.
- **Simultaneity:** in physics, simultaneity remains frame-dependent. An "all-at-once" reading may instead be formulated as a global constraint or boundary-value view; it does not establish a universal present.

The strongest unifying principle is therefore **typed integration without category collapse**.

## Physical action

Adopt metric signature `(-,+,+,+)` and the reduced Planck mass

\[
M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}=8\pi G/c^4
\]

when stress-energy is expressed in SI-compatible units. In natural units `c=hbar=1`, the minimal scalar-tensor seed is

\[
S_{\rm P}=\int_{\mathcal M} d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[
\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2}{2}(R-2\Lambda)
-\frac12 Z(\phi)\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla^\mu\phi
-V(\phi)
+\sum_i \frac{c_i}{\Lambda_\star^{d_i-4}}\mathcal O_i
\right]+S_{\rm SM}[A^2(\phi)g_{\mu\nu},\psi].
\]

Here:

- `phi` is a candidate scalar extension, not consciousness by definition;
- `Z(phi)>0` controls the kinetic term;
- `V(phi)` is a declared potential;
- `A(phi)` specifies a conformal matter coupling;
- `O_i` are symmetry-allowed higher-dimensional operators;
- `Lambda_*` is the effective-theory cutoff;
- `c_i` are Wilson coefficients with priors and bounds.

This is a conventional, deliberately modest kernel. It gives the GMUT programme somewhere mathematically legitimate to begin.

## Field equations and the Omega term

Varying with respect to `g^{mu nu}` gives

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}\left(
T^{\rm SM}_{\mu\nu}+T^{\phi}_{\mu\nu}+T^{\rm EFT}_{\mu\nu}
\right),
\]

with

\[
T^{\phi}_{\mu\nu}
=Z(\phi)\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi
-g_{\mu\nu}\left[\frac12 Z(\phi)(\nabla\phi)^2+V(\phi)\right].
\]

The evidence-bounded Mandala term is a definition:

\[
\boxed{
\Omega_{\mu\nu}
:=M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}\left(T^{\phi}_{\mu\nu}+T^{\rm EFT}_{\mu\nu}\right)
}
\]

so that

\[
\boxed{
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}T^{\rm SM}_{\mu\nu}+\Omega_{\mu\nu}.
}
\]

This is the recommended **Mandala Field Equation**. It does not claim that Omega is a newly discovered force. It packages a specified extension sector whose action, dimensions, conservation law, and observables remain inspectable.

The earlier form

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi T_{\mu\nu}+\Psi_{\mu\nu}
\]

is recovered in geometric units `G=c=1` by identifying `Psi_{mu nu}=Omega_{mu nu}` and defining what is included in `T`. In ordinary units the matter coefficient is `8 pi G/c^4`, not bare `8 pi`.

If `Psi_{mu nu}` is intended to represent consciousness, the symbol alone is insufficient. A separate field or operational variable, action, coupling, units, source, observable, existing bound, and falsifier would all be required. No such empirically supported construction is assumed here.

## Scalar equation and exchange

Define

\[
\beta(\phi):=M_{\rm Pl}\frac{d\ln A}{d\phi}.
\]

With the sign convention used in this project, variation with respect to `phi` gives

\[
Z\Box\phi+\frac12 Z_{,\phi}(\nabla\phi)^2-V_{,\phi}
=-\frac{\beta(\phi)}{M_{\rm Pl}}T_{\rm SM}+\mathcal J_{\rm EFT}.
\]

The contracted Bianchi identity requires total conservation:

\[
\nabla^\mu\left(T^{\rm SM}_{\mu\nu}+T^{\phi}_{\mu\nu}+T^{\rm EFT}_{\mu\nu}\right)=0.
\]

Individual sectors may exchange four-momentum,

\[
\nabla^\mu T^{\rm SM}_{\mu\nu}=Q_\nu,
\qquad
\nabla^\mu(T^{\phi}_{\mu\nu}+T^{\rm EFT}_{\mu\nu})=-Q_\nu,
\]

where the minimal conformal coupling gives

\[
Q_\nu=\frac{\beta(\phi)}{M_{\rm Pl}}T_{\rm SM}\nabla_\nu\phi
\]

up to the declared stress-tensor convention. The sign convention, not just the prose, must be shared by the action, equations, and code.

## The AB equation

The historical expression

\[
\mathcal G_{AB}=8\pi\mathcal T_{AB}+\alpha\Omega_{AB}
\]

has two legitimate uses:

1. **Physical extended-space equation.** Then `A,B` index a declared manifold or bundle with dimension, signature, connection, curvature, action, symmetries, compactification/projection map, and observables. The coefficient `alpha` must carry whatever units make every term agree.
2. **Ontology map.** Then `A,B` label physical, informational, and ethical registers. The equation is a diagram of relations, not Einstein's equation and not evidence of a new field.

Until the first construction exists, this project uses the second interpretation and requires the projection gate

\[
\Pi_{\rm P}:\mathfrak M_{\rm P\oplus I\oplus E}\longrightarrow
\left(G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
-M_{\rm Pl}^{-2}T^{\rm SM}_{\mu\nu}-\Omega_{\mu\nu}\right)=0.
\]

No informational or ethical term passes `Pi_P` unless it is mapped to a physical field or control variable with units and empirical support.

## Null recovery

The minimum recovery condition is

\[
A(\phi)\to1,\quad \nabla_\mu\phi\to0,\quad
V(\phi)\to V_0,\quad c_i/\Lambda_\star^{d_i-4}\to0.
\]

Absorb constant `V_0` into `Lambda`. Then `Omega_{mu nu}` vanishes or becomes a cosmological-constant shift and the theory recovers GR plus the Standard Model in its stated domain.
