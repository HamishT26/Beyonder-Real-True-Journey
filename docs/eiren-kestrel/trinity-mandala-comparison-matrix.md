# Trinity Mandala comparison matrix

## Evidence scale

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| E0 | Metaphor, aspiration, spiritual interpretation, or design fiction |
| E1 | Project record or internally coherent proposal |
| E2 | Reproducible internal artifact, unit test, simulation, or repository workflow |
| E3 | Independent benchmark, standards conformance, adversarial evaluation, or pilot evidence |
| E4 | Peer-reviewed evidence, externally replicated result, or governed production deployment |
| E5 | Mature scientific, engineering, or legal consensus within a stated domain |

Grades are claim-specific. A system can have E4 software reliability evidence and only E0 evidence for consciousness.

## The larger mosaic

The strongest next-level interpretation is a **three-register research constitution**:

1. **Physical register:** quantities with dimensions, dynamics, conservation, uncertainty, and empirical falsifiers.
2. **Informational/operational register:** algorithms, complexity metrics, memory, coordination, reliability, and resource costs.
3. **Normative/contemplative register:** rights, consent, care, meaning, spiritual interpretation, and cultural authority.

The registers may constrain and inform one another, but they do not become interchangeable. A moral value is not stress-energy; a Shannon bit is not subjective meaning; a fluent model output is not proof of experience; and an elegant equation is not legal legitimacy. The Mandala becomes stronger—not weaker—when these interfaces are typed.

## Overall verdict

The GHC work is genuinely unusual in breadth and unusually mature in operational continuity. Its leading edge is not yet a new law of physics or ASI. It is the emerging combination of:

- an action-first candidate physics kernel,
- a reproducible multi-lane research-operations system,
- a rights-and-consent constitutional layer, and
- an explicit spiritual/pluralist meaning layer.

That combination is worth developing. The accurate public claim today is: **a distinctive interdisciplinary research and governance programme with working orchestration prototypes and testable physical seeds**.
