# Module 04: Pillars practices and evidence limits

## Pillars and practices



The **measurement-model analysis** practice belongs primarily to GMUT Mind. It asks whether a quantity, unit, correction, uncertainty term, comparison, and falsifier are specified precisely enough to be checked. A synthetic rational calculation can expose ambiguity, but it cannot measure a field, calibrate an instrument, estimate a physical parameter, or confirm the GMUT scalar-tensor and effective-field-theory research family.

The **experimental-design planning** practice belongs primarily to THOS Body. It makes blocks, Latin squares, factorial combinations, sample indices, and comparison assumptions inspectable. These structures are plans and model inputs. They are not actual randomization, participant assignment, laboratory work, operational effectiveness, deployment readiness, or evidence of AGI or ASI.

The **reproducible-computation engineering** practice also contributes to THOS Body. Exact rationals, full typed envelopes, immutable source digests, hash-locked wheels, explicit failures, and deterministic runners reduce avoidable ambiguity. They do not create independent reproduction because the definitions, implementations, tests, packages, and validation all remain under the same owner and shared infrastructure.

The **provenance, accessibility, and rights stewardship** practice belongs to Freed ID and CBR Heart. It separates digest correspondence from authorship, consent, ownership, legal validity, cultural legitimacy, trust, identity, and affected-party approval. The accessible projections have structure, headings, tables, source links, and plain-language boundaries; manual browser, keyboard, screen-reader, magnification, voice-control, cognitive, language, and affected-user evaluation remain open.

The recommended practice for Elaren is **synthetic calibration-drift and interlaboratory comparison registration**. It should retain reference hierarchy, observation times, uncertainty budgets, transfer standards, missingness, covariance, operator and environment placeholders, and disagreement without converting them into real results or competence.
