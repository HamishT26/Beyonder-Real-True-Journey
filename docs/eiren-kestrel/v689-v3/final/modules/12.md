# Module 12: Scientific governance and authority boundaries

## Scientific governance and authority boundaries



The phase supports three methodological candidates rather than fundamental laws. First, a **comparison sufficiency principle**: no resource, model, or intervention comparison is interpretable without a matched workload, result-quality definition, settings, sampling rule, stopping rule, and attributable denominator. Second, an **uncertainty provenance principle**: every reported uncertainty needs its components, sensitivity model, dependence assumptions, unit domain, correction state, and scope. Third, an **authority nonpromotion principle**: a complete computational record cannot manufacture the consent, competence, legitimacy, or public authority missing from its inputs.

These principles are useful engineering and governance heuristics. They are not new laws of thermodynamics, psychology, consciousness, nature, religion, society, or reality. The software does not define a measurable psyche variable, a physical reservoir, a GMUT observable, a calibrated likelihood, or an experiment capable of distinguishing GMUT from established theories. The calculations therefore neither confirm nor refute GMUT and do not support a Theory-of-Everything claim.

THOS remains synthetic and proxy-only. A task schedule, runner, skill catalogue, package environment, finite statistical calculation, or task handoff does not establish general intelligence, autonomous agency, ASI, embodiment, operational effectiveness, safety, or production readiness. Freed ID remains nonproduction without standards-conformant live keys and proofs, issuance, resolution, status, revocation, interoperability, independent privacy and security review, recovery evidence, trust governance, and affected-party oversight.

CBR, professional metrology, workplace and laboratory safety, research ethics, participant governance, publication, privacy remedy, accessibility accommodation, legal interpretation, cultural interpretation, traditional knowledge, Maori wording, Maori concepts, Maori data governance, tangata whenua, iwi, hapu, and Maori authority remain with competent and affected people and authorities. Maori concepts remain under Maori authority.
