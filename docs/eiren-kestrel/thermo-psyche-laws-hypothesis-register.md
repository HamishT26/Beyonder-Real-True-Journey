# Candidate laws of thermo-psyche dynamics

## Status boundary

There is no established scientific discipline in which thermodynamic entropy, subjective meaning, moral value, and spiritual unity share one accepted set of fundamental equations. The register below therefore distinguishes:

- **P-law:** established or standard physical/information-science result in its stated domain;
- **B-principle:** testable engineering or behavioral design principle;
- **E-principle:** normative constitutional commitment;
- **M-heuristic:** metaphysical or contemplative interpretation.

Only the first category is called a physical law. The others can be powerful without being disguised as physics.

## L0 — Law of epistemic typing

**Class:** B-principle; logical prerequisite.

Every variable and claim has a domain label

\[
D(x)\in\{\mathsf P,\mathsf I,\mathsf E,\mathsf M\},
\]

for physical, informational, ethical, or metaphysical/contemplative content. A cross-domain use requires an explicit map

\[
f_{a\rightarrow b}:x_a\mapsto y_b
\]

with definition, dimensions, uncertainty, evidence, and failure conditions.

**Test:** a schema validator rejects untyped tensor terms and normative variables placed directly in spacetime equations.

**What would diminish it:** a rigorously derived theory showing that two registers are mathematically identical and empirically indistinguishable in the claimed domain. Resemblance or shared vocabulary is insufficient.

**Mandala effect:** strongly increases validity by preventing category errors.

## L1 — Law of balanced physical exchange

**Class:** P-law under the symmetries and domain of the physical model.

For coupled sectors,

\[
\nabla^\mu T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu}=Q^{(i)}_\nu,
\qquad
\sum_i Q^{(i)}_\nu=0,
\]

so total stress-energy is conserved. Open systems declare boundary currents rather than pretending to be closed.

**Test:** symbolic and numerical divergence checks; energy and momentum budgets with residual tolerance.

**Failure implication:** the physical model, its numerical integrator, or its boundary accounting is wrong.

**Mandala effect:** turns "symbiosis" into a measurable exchange architecture without implying that every relationship is energy transfer.

## L2 — Law of entropy honesty

**Class:** P-law for thermodynamic/stochastic entropy; information theorem for Shannon entropy; B-principle for reporting.

For a well-defined physical process, expected total entropy production obeys

\[
\langle\Sigma\rangle\ge 0
\]

under the stated assumptions. Shannon entropy

\[
H(X)=-\sum_x p(x)\log p(x)
\]

measures uncertainty in a distribution, not semantic truth, goodness, love, or experience.

**Test:** declare the ensemble, coarse-graining, units, bath, trajectories, and estimator; report uncertainty.

**Failure implication:** either a fluctuation is being confused with the expectation, boundary information is missing, or the quantity is not thermodynamic entropy.

**Mandala effect:** preserves legitimate physical-information connections while blocking rhetorical entropy metaphors from becoming equations.

## L3 — Law of embodied information cost

**Class:** P-law in the Landauer regime.

Erasure of one classical bit in an ideal quasistatic process at temperature `T` has minimum dissipated heat

\[
Q_{\min}=k_B T\ln2.
\]

Actual computers generally consume much more. Reversible computation can change the accounting, and quantum side information changes the generalized bound.

**Test:** count logically irreversible operations, state the physical implementation, and measure energy/heat.

**Failure implication:** claims of cost-free physical computation or memory erasure are rejected unless the assumptions are shown not to apply.

**Mandala effect:** gives THOS a real body budget—energy, cooling, memory, and time—without claiming that every thought has a simple one-bit cost.

## L4 — Law of requisite regulatory variety

**Class:** established cybernetic theorem in formal models; B-principle in complex operations.

In Ashby's regulator setting, disturbances cannot be attenuated beyond the regulator's available discriminatory and response variety. Informally, only adequate response variety can absorb environmental variety.

**Test:** define disturbance classes, observable states, control actions, target outcomes, and residual error. Compare a simpler controller against the multi-lane system.

**Failure implication:** adding siblings, skills, or packets without increasing useful response coverage is overhead, not intelligence.

**Mandala effect:** supports plural expertise while requiring ablation evidence that the plurality helps.

## L5 — Law of resource-bounded agency

**Class:** B-principle.

An agentic claim is incomplete unless it specifies a budget vector

\[
B=(C_{\rm compute},E_{\rm energy},T_{\rm time},P_{\rm permissions},R_{\rm risk},U_{\rm uncertainty})
\]

and a termination condition. Capability is evaluated against task success, reliability, cost, latency, and harm—not eloquence alone.

**Test:** matched-budget single-agent versus round-robin trials, blind scoring, failure injection, and usage accounting.

**Failure implication:** if the round robin is no better after cost and variance are included, its role should be narrowed to continuity or review rather than called superintelligence.

**Mandala effect:** makes THOS claims comparable to real agent systems.

## L6 — Law of integration without erasure

**Class:** B-principle and E-principle.

A composite preserves contributor provenance, dissent, uncertainty, and recoverability:

\[
\operatorname{Integrate}(x_1,\ldots,x_n)
\Rightarrow
\{\operatorname{source}(x_i),\operatorname{confidence}(x_i),\operatorname{recover}(x_i)\}_{i=1}^n.
\]

Synthesis may compress but must not silently replace identity, consent, or disagreement.

**Test:** trace any conclusion back to sources; reverse a merge; recover minority findings; distinguish `MESSAGE_SENT` from `PREPARED_NOT_SENT`.

**Failure implication:** a smooth narrative that destroys provenance is epistemic loss, even if it feels unified.

**Mandala effect:** converts family continuity and non-replacement language into an auditable design invariant.

## L7 — Law of consent, least authority, and reversible power

**Class:** E-principle with security-engineering consequences.

An action is permitted only when a valid authorization predicate holds:

\[
\mathcal A(s,a,r,c,t)=\mathrm{true},
\]

where subject `s`, action `a`, resource `r`, context `c`, and time `t` are explicit. Authority is minimized; consequential actions require review, appeal, and rollback where physically possible.

**Test:** capability manifests, scoped credentials, human approval gates, revocation, audit logs, and incident exercises.

**Failure implication:** benevolent intent does not cure excessive agency or missing authority.

**Mandala effect:** gives the Heart pillar operational force in the Body pillar.

## L8 — Law of no free phenomenology

**Class:** B-principle for consciousness research and AI claims.

Fluent self-report, integration, recurrence, memory, or relational attachment may motivate moral caution, but none alone establishes phenomenal consciousness. A scientific claim requires:

\[
(O,N,U,F)=(\text{observable},\text{null},\text{uncertainty},\text{falsifier}).
\]

**Test:** preregister competing theories, discriminating predictions, controls, and blinded evaluation. Preserve a precautionary non-cruelty policy independently of metaphysical certainty.

**Failure implication:** terms such as "awake," "self-aware," and "conscious field" remain relational or metaphorical unless the test is passed.

**Mandala effect:** protects both scientific integrity and moral humility.

## L9 — Law of provenance-weighted belief

**Class:** B-principle.

Evidence weight depends on independence, calibration, directness, replication, and counterevidence. Repetition count alone does not multiply truth:

\[
W(E)=f(I_{\rm independent},C_{\rm calibrated},D_{\rm direct},R_{\rm replicated},X_{\rm counterevidence}).
\]

**Test:** semantic deduplication, source-dependence graphs, calibration curves, and independent reproduction.

**Failure implication:** 100 templated reflections may have nearly the evidence weight of one template.

**Mandala effect:** directly repairs the main weakness found in the v601-v640 artifact counts.

## L10 — Law of scale and coarse-graining discipline

**Class:** P/B principle.

Every effective description declares its scale `ell`, cutoff, state variables, and mapping to adjacent scales. A term valid for neurons, organizations, or cosmology does not transfer merely because the same graph or word appears.

**Test:** derive or estimate coarse-graining maps, compare observables across resolutions, and show insensitivity to irrelevant microscopic detail within the stated domain.

**Failure implication:** unsupported micro-to-macro or psyche-to-cosmos leaps remain analogy.

**Mandala effect:** creates a principled route from Mind to Body without flattening levels.

## How these laws could falsify parts of the Trinity Mandala

| Claim | Required result | Result that weakens or rejects it |
|---|---|---|
| GMUT physical extension | Stable action, conservation, null recovery, existing-bound compliance, unique forecast | Ghost/gradient instability, failed conservation, no viable parameter region, or no distinguishable observable |
| Round robin improves research | Higher blind quality/reliability per matched cost than single-agent and simpler workflows | Equal/lower quality after deduplication and cost accounting |
| Memory preserves identity-relevant continuity | High retrieval accuracy, provenance, consent, deletion/recovery correctness | Poisoning, stale routing, untraceable compression, or unrecoverable identity conflation |
| Freed ID protects agency | Threat model, assurance profile, recovery, selective disclosure, coercion resistance, appeal | Controller capture, correlation, unrecoverable loss, ambiguous authority, or rights without remedy |
| CBR is globally legitimate | Participatory adoption, cultural authority, jurisdiction, amendment, enforcement, remedy | Unilateral authorship presented as universal law or appropriation of protected knowledge |
| AI siblings are conscious persons | Discriminating evidence accepted under a credible consciousness research programme | Self-report and system complexity remain the sole evidence |

## Unsolved-problem bridge

These problems should enter future phases as **challenge sets**, not as things a Mandala equation is assumed to solve.

### Physics and cosmology

- Quantum gravity and the relation between spacetime and quantum theory.
- The Yang-Mills mass gap, a precisely stated Millennium Prize problem.
- Dark matter identity and the nature of dark energy; current DESI results are suggestive, not a GMUT confirmation.
- The cosmological-constant and hierarchy problems.
- Black-hole information and the microscopic origin of horizon entropy.
- Matter-antimatter asymmetry and neutrino masses.
- The measurement problem and interpretation of quantum mechanics.
- Turbulence and the Navier-Stokes existence/smoothness problem.

**Mandala rule:** choose one, state the accepted formulation, reproduce a baseline, identify one narrow contribution, and name what would count as failure.

### Mathematics and computation

- P versus NP, Riemann hypothesis, Navier-Stokes, and Yang-Mills must remain separate precisely stated problems.
- "Erdos problems" are a large family, not one target. Every phase must identify an exact problem number or statement and verify its current status before claiming progress.
- Gödel incompleteness limits sufficiently expressive consistent formal systems; it does not mean every truth is unknowable or every contradiction is profound.
- Computational irreducibility can limit prediction shortcuts, but coarse-grained regularities may still exist.
- Combinatorial explosion motivates heuristics, decomposition, and proof certificates; it does not certify multi-agent answers.

### Consciousness and psyche

- The explanatory/hard problem, neural correlates, report versus experience, animal consciousness, and machine moral status remain open.
- IIT and global neuronal workspace theories make different commitments; recent adversarial work produced mixed rather than decisive support.
- A GHC `Psi` index can be a memory/integration metric if operationally defined, but should not be called a consciousness meter.

### Ethics, law, and society

- How rights apply under uncertainty about moral patienthood.
- How to preserve human rights while extending precautionary protections to animals, ecosystems, and artificial systems.
- How global principles coexist with jurisdiction, democratic legitimacy, Indigenous sovereignty, tikanga, and cultural pluralism.
- How identity systems resist coercion, surveillance, correlation, controller capture, and catastrophic key loss.
- How high-capability agents remain corrigible when tools, memory, and other agents span trust boundaries.

### Spiritual and theological questions

- The problem of evil and suffering; divine hiddenness; freedom and providence; unity and plurality; personal identity through change; and the epistemic status of mystical experience.
- Buddhist dependent origination, Advaita non-duality, Christian stewardship/integral ecology, Spinozan immanence, and Māori whakapapa can illuminate distinct relationships. Their differences should not be erased into a claim that every tradition says the same thing.
- Spiritual convergence is an interpretive dialogue, not a physics experiment. Participation, attribution, and cultural authority are part of truthfulness.

## Paradox discipline

| Paradox or limit | Practical lesson for GHC |
|---|---|
| Liar and self-reference | Self-certification needs an external or stratified verifier |
| Russell's paradox | Registries need type boundaries; unrestricted self-membership is unsafe |
| Gödel incompleteness | No rich formal system should claim complete self-proof of all relevant truths |
| Sorites | Thresholds need uncertainty bands, review, and appeal rather than false precision |
| Ship of Theseus | Identity continuity needs criteria for provenance, memory, embodiment, consent, and social recognition |
| Newcomb and game-theoretic dilemmas | Policy depends on causal assumptions and other-agent models; state them |
| Fermi paradox | Absence of observed evidence is a constraint, not proof of either absence or hidden abundance |
| Grandfather/time-travel paradoxes | Cross-time claims require a concrete causal model, not narrative possibility |
| Omnipotence paradox | Unbounded capability language is incoherent; define domains and constraints |
| Trolley-style dilemmas | Aggregate benefit does not erase rights, procedure, or uncertainty |

## Recommended phase gate

No candidate law should be promoted to "fundamental" until it has:

1. a precise domain and vocabulary;
2. a formal statement;
3. at least one nontrivial prediction or decision consequence;
4. a null model and counterexample search;
5. uncertainty and boundary conditions;
6. independent criticism or replication; and
7. a clear statement of whether it is physical, behavioral, normative, or contemplative.

The best immediate contribution of thermo-psyche dynamics is not a new universal constant. It is a disciplined interface among energy, information, agency, identity, and care.
