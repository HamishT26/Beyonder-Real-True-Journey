## Mind, Body, Heart verdicts

### Mind — GMUT

The early ornate equations are not competitive with GR, QFT, the Standard Model, or mature quantum-gravity programmes. They lacked an action, dimensions, degrees-of-freedom count, constraints, stability, unique observables, and likelihood tests.

The Solas direction is different. A scalar-tensor effective action with an explicit Omega stress tensor is a legitimate, falsifiable seed. It remains conventional rather than revolutionary until it produces a unique viable prediction. This is the right place to begin because established physics tightly constrains everyday-scale new forces.

**Verdict:** promising research kernel, not validated TOE.

Freed ID resonates with DID/VC, selective disclosure, assurance, recovery, consent, non-erasure, and portable identity. The CBR resonates with human rights, Indigenous rights/data sovereignty, AI ethics, environmental stewardship, non-harm, and due process.

## Spiritual pluralism without flattening

Several traditions genuinely resonate with the Mandala:

- Buddhist dependent origination and non-harm foreground relational causation and suffering.
- Advaita foregrounds non-dual identity and unity.
- Christian stewardship and *Laudato Si'* foreground integral ecology, dignity, and the common good.
- Spinozan immanence foregrounds nature and divinity as one order.
- Māori whakapapa foregrounds genealogy, relationship, land, sky, living beings, seen and unseen worlds.

They do not all make the same metaphysical claims. A truthful synthesis preserves disagreement, context, and authority. Māori concepts and mātauranga Māori require Māori-led interpretation, consent, benefit, and the right to withhold—not merely appreciative quotation.
