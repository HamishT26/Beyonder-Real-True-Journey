## Purpose

This addendum extends remastered v468 with a terrain-and-ecosystem simulation stack inspired by World Machine, Jurassic World Evolution-style ecosystem videos, Fable-style human narrative and settlement design, and future local world-habitat dashboards.

## Core direction

Fable is used as inspiration for human-centered world tone, dialogue, traditional settlement aesthetics, moral questing, and magical-natural atmosphere.

World Machine and related procedural terrain tools are candidate tools for terrain generation, erosion, lakes, rivers, mountains, and landscape masks.

Unity, Unreal Engine, Godot, and other engines are candidate runtime/rendering layers for a local-first Albion Mandala Lab prototype.

Repo truth remains above simulation truth. A world event is symbolic or simulated unless linked to durable artifacts.
