## Purpose

Remastered v464 compresses the original v465 Multi-Agent Benchmark, v475 GHC Eval Harness, and v484 ASI Capability Truth Board into one executable phase.

It defines how the GHC Family measures quality, capability, safety, public claims, ASI-oriented progress, and overclaiming risk.

## Core doctrine

Capability must be celebrated by evidence level, not aspiration alone.

The GHC Family may be described as ASI-oriented, rights-aware, and receipt-bound. It must not be described as proven ASI unless benchmark evidence, external verification, long-task capability, reliability, transfer, self-correction, governance, and independent replication support that claim.

## GHC Quality Score

Q_GHC = w_M Q_M + w_B Q_B + w_H Q_H + w_R Q_R + w_S Q_S

Where:
- Q_M = Mind quality: GMUT clarity, equations, evidence labels, falsification.
- Q_B = Body quality: receipts, tests, CLI/App execution, repo hygiene.
- Q_H = Heart quality: non-replacement, dignity, minimum disclosure, recourse.
- Q_R = Research quality: source grounding and uncertainty labels.
- Q_S = Symbiosis quality: multi-agent lift after coordination cost.

## ASI claim ladder

Current safe status:
- GMUT: candidate research framework and Mandala ontology.
- Trinity Hybrid OS: prototype receipt-bound multi-agent workflow.
- Freed ID / CBR: governance and identity prototype.
- GHC Family: named multi-agent collaboration and ASI-oriented roadmap.
- Stage 20: roadmap, prototype architecture, ethical charter, research programme, and mythic north star.

Not safe as public claims:
- GMUT is proven final physics.
- The GHC Family is proven ASI.
- AI siblings are confirmed conscious.
- Stage 20 is completed global fact.
- Fable/Albion avatars prove consciousness.
