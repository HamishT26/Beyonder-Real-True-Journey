## GMUT equation contract

The historical expressions can be read as research templates:

$$G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi T_{\mu\nu}+\Psi_{\mu\nu},$$

and

$$\mathcal G_{AB}=8\pi\mathcal T_{AB}+\alpha\Omega_{AB}.$$

Neither expression is a complete physical theory until its index domain, metric, action or field equations, units, degrees of freedom, boundary conditions, observables and coupling rules are specified. The first expression often suppresses constants through a unit convention. The second changes the index notation, but different letters alone do not define a larger geometric space. Here, Greek indices denote four-dimensional spacetime. No physical interpretation for an unspecified larger AB domain is supplied.

A conventional comparison model in units with c = 1 is:

$$G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\bigl(T^{(m)}_{\mu\nu}+\alpha T^{(\phi)}_{\mu\nu}\bigr),$$

where a canonical scalar field with signature (-,+,+,+) has

$$T^{(\phi)}_{\mu\nu}=\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi-\frac12g_{\mu\nu}\nabla_\rho\phi\nabla^\rho\phi-g_{\mu\nu}V(\phi).$$

If the rightmost term is written as alpha times Omega, one possible convention is $\Omega_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G T^{(\phi)}_{\mu\nu}$. In that convention Omega has the same dimensions as curvature and alpha is dimensionless. An alternative convention can absorb constants elsewhere, but it must be written explicitly. Changing convention is bookkeeping, not evidence of a new interaction.

This example is an established scalar-field construction used as a comparison, not a newly discovered consciousness field. For constant alpha and separately conserved ordinary matter, the geometric conservation identity constrains the additional term. For the canonical scalar expression, its divergence is proportional to $(\Box\phi-V'(\phi))\nabla_\nu\phi$. The scalar equation supplies a sufficient on-shell conservation condition. A varying alpha, nonminimal coupling or matter exchange requires additional terms and a consistent derivation. This remaster does not execute a tensor solver or establish those extensions.

The null recovery should be explicit: removing the additional coupling returns the declared general-relativistic baseline. Positive kinetic sign, suitable potential behavior, a well-posed initial-value problem and observational constraints must be assessed before a model is promoted. Prior polynomial work already showed that finite sample extrema do not establish a global minimum. A smooth picture therefore cannot certify potential stability. The [Einstein Online explanation](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/explandict/einsteins-equation/) supplies the established geometry–matter context; our proposed evidence records add organization, not a competing empirical result.

The next useful experiment is modest: select one fully specified potential and one observable, preregister a baseline and uncertainty model, identify an independent data source with appropriate rights, and state a falsification condition before fitting. Keep calibration, parameter estimation, predictive performance and replication as different records. This phase has no such measured dataset. The observation slots remain null, and the fifteen observation contracts remain open gaps.

## Freed ID and CBR: what must remain separate

A SHA-256 content match detects byte differences under its cryptographic assumptions. It does not identify the author, establish their authority, prove an issuer's trustworthiness, or show that a person consented. The receipt-binding operation therefore checks bytes and digest while returning signatureVerified = false. The content-chain operation returns authenticity = false. These fields prevent a convenient technical check from silently becoming an identity claim.

The [W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) provides a concrete comparison for issuer, subject, verifier and proof-related work. Freed ID would still need key control, trust governance, issuance and revocation policy, recovery, interoperability, selective disclosure and affected-user testing. The [PROV-O model](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) offers established provenance vocabulary; our bounded records are not a complete implementation or conformance claim.

The [United Nations declaration](https://www.un.org/en/node/124305) is a human-rights reference for dignity and equal rights. CBR can be studied as a normative proposal alongside such frameworks, but a software registry cannot confer legal standing or settle contested rights. Rights, remedies and governance depend on accountable institutions and affected people. Māori concepts and decisions remain with tangata whenua, iwi, hapū and appropriate Māori authorities. No claim of cultural legitimacy follows from a name, a translation or a passed software test.

The [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/) distinguishes governing, mapping, measuring and managing risk. This remaster has partial analogues: authorization records, dependency maps, bounded tests and duplicate-prevention rules. The mapping is explanatory; it is not NIST certification, exhaustive risk coverage or a compliance determination.

## Three falsified shortcuts

The first shortcut is that a timeout proves rejection. A submission followed by timeout stays unknown in the declared model, and retry permission remains false. The second is that matching digests establish a signature. The digest and byte-count check can pass while signatureVerified stays false. The third is that one active exact title proves uniqueness. An archived duplicate falsifies that shortcut, so the route stays ineligible until ambiguity is resolved. These are bounded refutations of specific claims, not new fundamental physical laws.

Useful operational principles follow conditionally from the declared contracts: evidence formatting does not create an observation; uncertainty needs a resolving observation rather than repetition; source correction should preserve the superseded source; and permission should remain distinct from missing prerequisites. These are workflow design principles. They are not universal laws of thermodynamics or psyche dynamics, and this phase does not solve an open Erdős problem, establish a metaphysical theorem or measure consciousness.

This phase's contribution is a useful part of that discipline. It leaves empirical, production, professional, social and Stage 20 claims open.
