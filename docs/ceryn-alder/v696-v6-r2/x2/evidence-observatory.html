What the evidence does and does not establish

These are bounded synthetic event traces, not measurements from the Codex service and not physical simulations. A timeout leaves delivery uncertain. A later authoritative observation may resolve it. An accepted message does not prove that a recipient started or completed its work.

The renderer uses floating-point trigonometry only to rotate exact integer coordinates. It sends no network requests and operates no tasks. It provides a table alternative, but no complete accessibility or independent validation claim is made.
