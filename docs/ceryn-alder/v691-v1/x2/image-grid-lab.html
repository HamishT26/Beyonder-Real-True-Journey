Three counterexamples to overclaiming
Dependence changes the question
Independent interval propagation of [-1,1] minus [-1,1] gives [-2,2]. If both symbols are the same x, the exact expression x − x is zero. The enclosure does not establish a calibrated measurement distribution.
A mean does not preserve a grid
The checkerboard [[1,-1],[-1,1]] and the zero grid both have a 2 × 2 block mean of zero. Matching summaries do not prove that source pixels were retained or that two images are identical.
Declarations leave authority reserved
