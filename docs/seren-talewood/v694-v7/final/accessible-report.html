What was checked

The phase contains 100 operation-case contracts, 47 invariant tests and 419 dimension-and-threshold-pair oracle comparisons. The oracle enumerates cycle and boundary cosets separately from the column-reduction implementation. These are checks by the same owner, not independent reproduction.

Why the conventions matter

A class is active at its birth and inactive at its death. A null death in the saved JSON means no death within the supplied filtration. Zero-length pairs are retained separately and do not count as living classes. A class in the highest supplied dimension is included.

Two barcodes can share the same Betti curve while having different relationships across thresholds. A different declared filtration can also change a barcode even when its final complex is unchanged.
