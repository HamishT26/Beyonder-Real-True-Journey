# Capacity evidence and the three pillars

Seren's primary focus in this remaster is THOS: turn the requested model cadence into a usable schedule, make resource assumptions visible, and reduce repeated data work. GMUT contributes mathematical questions and explicit falsification. Freed ID and CBR contribute the conditions under which a result may inform a decision affecting someone. These are complementary research and design roles. None supplies the missing evidence of another.

## THOS scheduling and measured work

The new cycle has thirty identities and forty-five positions. The Astra share is one third rather than one half. There are 196 Sol and 98 Astra assignments in the 294-position continuation. If a Sol run has unit cost and an otherwise matched Astra run has cost r, the older per-run average is (1+r)/2 and the new average is (2+r)/3. Relative savings are (r-1)/(3(r+1)). At r equal to 2, 3 and 4, this gives one ninth, one sixth and one fifth. These are scenarios derived from an assumption, not measured model billing or token consumption.

The local data-loading comparison measures a different, narrower improvement. Fifty record selections from the same byte buffer produced identical output identifiers using repeated JSON decoding and using one decode followed by indexed selection. Five paired timings were recorded with alternating execution order. The source bytes and every timing remain in scientific-results.json. No disk access, language-model request, output-quality judgment, energy sensor or prompt-cache observation was included. The result supports reusing a parsed source within a helper call. It does not predict the speed or token use of an entire phase.

The older Trinity OS architecture describes neuromorphic, classical and quantum components and an energy-management loop. This remaster implements none of those hardware claims. A queue timeline and CPU memory sample are useful orchestration inputs, but they do not establish an exotic energy conversion, a quantum advantage, biological function or ASI. The modular architecture benefits from explicit arrival times, dependency completion, indivisible work units and admission limits before any hardware experiment is considered.

## GMUT equation obligations

The exact historical origin/main registry points to latex/grand_mandala.tex, dated 16 March 2026. It explicitly calls its equation a proposal for internal comparison rather than empirical establishment. The field equation is

\[
\mathcal G_{AB}+\Omega^{(M)}_{AB}+\Omega^{(R)}_{AB}
=8\pi\mathcal T_{AB}+\alpha\Xi_{AB}.
\]

Here the source describes the Mandala term as a coupling proposal, the R term as residual comparator structure, and Xi as an unestablished bridge. The source Lagrangian lists GR, Standard Model, Mandala, Xi and coupling contributions. A list of contribution names does not yet define an action that can be varied. The source's phrase confirmed evidence refers to repository comparison traces and validator outputs; it must not be read as confirmation of a new physical interaction.

A useful prospective refinement is to define a residual tensor E_AB equal to the left side minus the right side. That is a bookkeeping definition, not an added force. Before fitting it, specify the spacetime dimension, metric signature, connection, tensor symmetries, units, action measure, dynamical fields, boundary terms and parameter domain. State whether geometric units are in use. All summands need compatible dimensions and index structure. If G_AB is the ordinary Einstein tensor of a Levi-Civita connection, the contracted Bianchi identity constrains the covariant divergence of the full proposed equation. A coupled model must derive its matter and additional-field exchange terms consistently; one cannot separately assume every contribution is conserved.

For a concrete test, choose one observable and a calibrated instrument model. Declare a baseline model, the added parameter, nuisance parameters, selection mechanism and likelihood. State a null limit that recovers the baseline and an observation that would count against the proposed addition. Fit and evaluate on appropriately separated data, report uncertainty and sensitivity to assumptions, and retain a negative result. This remaster supplies no such physical data or instrument mapping. It therefore records these as research obligations rather than successful physics tests. Carroll's introductory [general-relativity notes](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9712019) are a baseline reference for the geometric framework; the specific GMUT additions still need their own definition and derivation.

## Information loss and sequential evidence

A finite erasure channel gives a precise example of lost information. Start with two equally likely states. With probability epsilon, replace the state label with an erasure symbol; otherwise report it exactly. The joint distributions were constructed with rational arithmetic at five epsilon values. Retained mutual information is 1-epsilon bits, while the minimum zero-one classification error is epsilon/2. The result depends on the chosen prior, channel and loss. A summary can be sufficient for one question and inadequate for another. It would be unjustified to claim that arbitrary compression always preserves every relevant decision.

For the sequential example, the null is a conditional success probability of one half at each step, and the fixed alternative is three quarters. A success multiplies the likelihood ratio by 3/2 and a failure by 1/2. Enumerating every binary path through each horizon from one to ten gives a null expectation of one at every horizon. By horizon ten, the exact probability of ever reaching four is 33/256, below the corresponding one-quarter bound. This finite verification illustrates the assumptions; it is not a proof of every sequential procedure. The broader role of nonnegative martingales is discussed in the primary [anytime-valid inference paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.03167).

The counterexample draws one fair latent bit and repeats it four times. Every position is marginally fair, but later conditional probabilities are no longer one half. The all-one path reaches 81/16 and occurs with probability one half. A careless application of the one-quarter bound would therefore be wrong. Repeated outputs from shared data, shared infrastructure or a common hidden cause require an explicit dependence model. Counting them as independent confirmations would inflate evidence.

These models suggest candidate methodological principles for the Trinity Mandala: preserve the assumptions of an inference, retain the losses hidden by a summary, and disclose shared sources of agreement. They are not newly discovered fundamental laws of thermodynamics or psychology. Parrondo's [thermodynamics-of-information review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12447) concerns physical information processing. The [August 2026 computing-with-heat preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10027) is a recent research lead about finite thermal reservoirs; its abstract was reviewed, while its proofs and application to the present hardware remain unverified. Neither source provides a mapping from this software's abstract labels to consciousness or a measurable psyche variable.

## Number theory and the uncovered cases

The Erdős–Straus question asks whether every 4/n for integer n at least two is a sum of three positive unit fractions. The [April 2026 Chamberland paper](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/aa42/aa42.pdf) still presents the universal question as unsolved and studies a characterization involving primes. This remaster verifies three elementary construction families, without claiming a new construction or computational bound.

For even n=2k, use denominators k, k+1 and k(k+1). The last two fractions sum to 1/k, so the total is 2/k=4/n. For n=3k, use k, 4k and 12k; their reciprocal coefficients sum to 4/3. For n congruent to three modulo four, let a=(n+1)/4 and q=na. The residual after 1/a is 1/q, which splits into 1/(q+1)+1/[q(q+1)]. Each construction has positive integer denominators in its declared domain.

Over n=2 through 500, direct rational substitution verifies 416 witnesses. The remaining 83 inputs are explicitly listed as uncovered by this implementation. This is not a list of counterexamples to the conjecture: many uncovered values have known solutions, including five. The boundary is the chosen three-family method. A future investigation can compare additional residue constructions while preserving the distinction between a wider finite cover and a universal proof.

## Freed ID and CBR decisions

The historical Freed ID specification distinguishes identifier control from credential issuance and revocation, although some aspirational prose is broader than the mechanisms supplied. A holder's control of an identifier does not force every verifier to trust an issuer or a claim. A credential's status, a person's consent for a particular use, the authority of a reviewer, and a right of appeal are separate questions. [W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/) supplies a data-model reference; the local grant and review examples are not a production credential implementation.

The new synthetic consent join checks subject, declared purpose, expiry and revocation. Its positive result is explicitly model eligibility, with real consent verification false. The review model exposes decisions with no unconflicted reviewer. A person's appearance in a roster does not establish competence, independence, jurisdiction or affected-party acceptance. The paired resource comparator similarly refuses a saving claim when workload or result equivalence is not established.

The largest-remainder example gives a further reason to keep ethics explicit. With weights 1500, 1500, 900, 500, 500 and 200, allocations at twenty-five slots are 7, 7, 4, 3, 3 and 1. At twenty-six they are 8, 8, 5, 2, 2 and 1. Two shares decrease as the total rises. This known allocation paradox is reproduced exactly. It does not prove that all sharing policies are unfair, but it rejects a universal monotonicity claim for this method. The family schedule follows Hamish's declared order; it is not silently replaced by that allocation algorithm.

Christian ethical reflection can contribute questions about care, humility, solidarity and treatment of others. Religious meaning and scientific observables require different kinds of argument. This remaster does not claim to verify a religious doctrine, to infer divine authority from software results, or to settle disputes among traditions. Its concrete ethical contribution is to preserve consent, review, disagreement, correction and refusal in the workflow. NIST's [measurement guidance](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/measure/) also supports recording context and limitations; it does not prescribe the local loss table or confer public authority.
