# Module 11 Rights care and the limits of a local posture record

Freed ID and CBR work should make a proposed use easier to inspect and contest. It should not turn the existence of a credential-shaped object into consent, competence or authority. The local purpose/expiry join returns model eligibility and explicitly states that real consent was not verified. The review coverage model can expose a missing reviewer or a declared conflict, but it does not choose a legitimate reviewer for an affected person.

The historical specification contains an aspiration that an identity holder controls an identifier. Credential issuers and verifiers still have distinct responsibilities, including status and trust decisions. A holder's control of a key cannot force every other party to accept a claim. A person's agreement to archive something does not automatically authorize publication or another purpose. A revocation field, an expiry time and a proof of authorship are separate facts with different evidence requirements.

The family schedule is an authorized work order for existing tasks. It is not a legal identity registry, a professional credential system or evidence about subjective wellbeing. The local posture record must distinguish actual owner execution from the unobserved state of other tasks. A source document's use of words such as live, identity or family does not turn finite graph behavior into a statement about consciousness or personhood. Treat those relational terms respectfully while keeping their evidence limits clear.

Christian ethical reflection and other philosophical traditions can contribute questions about care, humility, responsibility and treatment of others. This bundle does not settle doctrinal claims or derive divine or public authority from software. Its concrete contribution is to preserve disagreement, review, refusal, correction and affected-party questions in the workflow. Physical evidence, formal derivation and religious interpretation require different kinds of support.

Do not use the number of proposals, a Devday invitation, the size of a document, a model's confidence or a shared infrastructure result as a substitute for the evidence required by a claim. The present artifacts improve the inspectability of the work. They do not establish a Theory of Everything, ASI, consciousness, complete security, universal accessibility or Stage 20 readiness. The final verdict remains NOT_READY_FOR_STAGE_20.

When a blocked subject becomes a concrete, authorized and properly supported task, record that change prospectively. Preserve the earlier blocked record and explain the new evidence or authority. Do not erase the past state or treat a broad project aspiration as an automatic release of every future action. Hamish's authorization already covers suitable owner work and the declared terminal route; another generic permission wait is unnecessary for that scope.
