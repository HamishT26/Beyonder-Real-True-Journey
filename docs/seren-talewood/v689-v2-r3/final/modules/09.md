# Module 09 Scientific results and what they leave unresolved

The strongest lesson from the scientific work is that the assumptions belong with the result. Five finite erasure channels were constructed from an equally likely binary state. At erasure probability epsilon, retained information is 1-epsilon bits and the minimum zero-one error is epsilon/2. Exact rational joint probabilities support the calculation, while logarithms use an explicitly numerical display. This is a finite model of lost labels, not a physical or cognitive observation.

The sequential example enumerated every binary path at each horizon from one through ten, for 2,046 paths across the ten separate horizon calculations. Under the declared conditional fair-coin null, the expected final likelihood ratio is exactly one. The probability of ever reaching four by horizon ten is 33/256, below one quarter. These values are inspectable consequences of the finite model. The broader mathematics of anytime-valid inference is discussed in the primary source linked in x2/pillar-research.md; this software does not implement a universal inference engine.

The correlated counterexample repeats one fair latent bit four times. Each observation position is marginally fair, yet the conditional-null assumption fails after the first observation. The all-one path reaches 81/16 and has probability one half. This does not contradict the correctly conditional theorem. It shows why shared data, dependence or common hidden causes cannot be ignored when interpreting repeated agreement as evidence. Same-infrastructure model outputs are not automatically independent confirmations.

The number-theory exercise uses three elementary construction families for the Erdős–Straus equation. For even n=2k, denominators k, k+1 and k(k+1) give the required sum. For n=3k, denominators k, 4k and 12k work. For n congruent to three modulo four, a=(n+1)/4 and q=na reduce the remaining fraction to 1/q, which splits into 1/(q+1) and 1/[q(q+1)]. Direct exact substitution verified 416 witnesses in the range two through five hundred. Eighty-three integers remain uncovered by those chosen formulas.

The uncovered list is not a list of counterexamples to the general conjecture. It contains values with known solutions, including five. The exercise establishes the finite construction results and their domains; it does not improve the known computational bound or prove the universal statement. The current primary paper and the precise source-reading scope are recorded in the pillar research file. Future work should preserve this distinction while adding or comparing additional methods.

The historical GMUT equation registry describes an internally formalized proposal rather than empirical establishment. A useful next step is to define each tensor term, its dimensions, the action, the baseline limit, an observable and a falsifier. The residual tensor proposed in the research note is a bookkeeping definition of left side minus right side. It does not introduce a discovered interaction. Conditional consistency with ordinary geometric identities must be derived in an explicitly specified model. No calibrated physical observations, new force, quantum completion or independent reproduction is supplied by this bundle.

The potential Thermo/Psyche principles considered here are methodological candidates: preserve inference assumptions, expose information loss and disclose shared sources of agreement. They are not presented as new laws of thermodynamics or psychology. A recent computing-with-heat preprint is a research lead about finite thermal reservoirs, with proof and hardware applicability unverified in this run. An abstract state label does not supply a measurable psyche variable or a demonstration of consciousness.
