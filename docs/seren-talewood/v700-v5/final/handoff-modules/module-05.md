## 05. Planning, inherited review and proposal accounting

Planning selected three hundred Caelen records, fifteen from each of the twenty source operation groups. Their exact definitions, original identifiers, source files and definition hashes remain attached to the reviews. The transferable requirements concern typed domains, exact comparison, preserved uncertainty, immutable evidence and explicit authority limits. Tropical numerical results are not transferred into binary arithmetic. These inherited records earn zero new novelty and zero automatic completion credit.

Six hundred new case definitions cover twenty operation families across thirty profiles. Their hypotheses, malformed-input falsifiers, source needs, recovery instructions, protected gates and expected dispositions were frozen before execution. Six hundred case definitions must not be reported as six hundred scientific discoveries. The title audit compares the new cases against the six hundred accessible predecessor titles and records known overlap with existing Hamming and binary-matroid tools. It does not establish universal novelty across all historical proposals or the literature.

Each session contains three hundred safe envelope checks, three hundred malformed candidate attempts and three hundred disposable CLEAN/FIX/REFINE checks. Candidate rejection and successful refusal are distinct: a malformed subject retains zero completion credit even when its guard succeeds. Fifty exact-action packets and thirty blocked packets remain unexecuted because their concrete target or prerequisites are absent. Those packet counts are classifications of proposed actions, not additional completed core cases. Planning, x1, x2 and final are separate commits and must remain distinguishable in the lifecycle record.
