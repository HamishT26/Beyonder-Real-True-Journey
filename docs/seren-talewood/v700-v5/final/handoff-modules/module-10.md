## 10. Method Flow, failures and cumulative accounting

Read `final/method-flow-final.json` as a component index, not as a monolithic v1 ledger. The referenced x1 ledger includes planning. The referenced x2 components contain only their own delta. The final component contains closeout observations. Each component is validated with the shared v1 Method Flow validator; the final index also checks identifier uniqueness across the union. This avoids double counting planning, keeps documents below the ceiling and preserves every original witness in its exact source component.

Caelen's repository-effective baseline was 4,272 methods, 117,053 witnesses, 90,760 passes, 26,293 failures and 35,127 negatives. Canonical and pre-delivery route observations increased the pre-delivery baseline to 4,274 methods and 117,102 witnesses. A separate acknowledged-delivery layer adds one method and eight passing witnesses. The verified terminal baseline entering Seren is therefore 4,275 methods, 117,110 witnesses, 90,815 passes, 26,295 failures, 35,129 negatives, 1,109 open gaps and 1,195 exact gates. Fold that complete terminal baseline once.

Failure counters include deliberately invalid subjects as well as operational failures, with their categories stated. A successful refusal does not rehabilitate its invalid subject. An earlier failed display, wrong path, locator or control state remains failed even after recovery. Counts are bookkeeping over declared evidence records, not a universal measure of intelligence, reliability or scientific progress. External Seren canonical, route and delivery overlays must remain separate from the immutable repository count and be folded only when their actual receipts exist.
