## 11. The three pillars and the limits of this contribution

THOS Body was the primary focus: exact inputs, deterministic finite operations, complete responses, reversible changes, staged provenance and usable interfaces. These features improve the inspectability of a software research system. They do not demonstrate AGI, ASI, autonomous professional competence or a production hybrid operating system. A realistic deployment would require requirements, real workloads, adversarial evaluation, operational ownership, recovery procedures and independent review that this synthetic phase does not supply.

GMUT Mind benefits from the same discipline of declared objects and falsifiers. The character-sum argument is a mathematical identity on finite binary spaces. It cannot establish a law of thermodynamics, psyche dynamics, consciousness or the physical universe. A proposed physical interpretation would need a typed mapping to measurable quantities, a competing null model, identifiable parameters, calibrated observations and reproducible predictions. A spiritual or philosophical analogy may remain meaningful to its author while retaining a different evidential status from a scientific measurement.

Freed ID and CBR Heart are served here by visible uncertainty, preservation of competing answers, correction lineage and explicit limits on authority. A nonzero syndrome is not an identity judgment; a codeword match is not a credential proof; a successful correction is not consent. Real identity systems need governed keys, proofs, lifecycle, interoperability, security, recovery and affected-party oversight. Legal and cultural interpretation require competent authority. Maori concepts, wording and data governance remain under Maori authority. The phase's exact terminal readiness statement remains NOT_READY_FOR_STAGE_20.
