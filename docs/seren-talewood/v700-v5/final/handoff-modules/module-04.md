## 04. What the finite mathematics means

The input is a systematic binary generator matrix of the form [I | P], with two through eight columns and at most five rows. Every bit is an actual integer zero or one; Boolean values are rejected. Thirty distinct matrices cover different lengths, dimensions and parity blocks, including small repetition and Hamming examples. These examples are established mathematics. This phase contributes bounded implementations, comparisons, records and interfaces rather than claiming the discovery of linear codes or the MacWilliams identity.

The primary runner represents a binary vector as an integer, uses XOR for addition, and computes parity with integer population counts. The reference uses explicit lists of bits, integer sums modulo two and exhaustive vector enumeration. Minimum distance is compared through nonzero weights and pairwise distances. A systematic parity-check matrix is checked against orthogonality and exact membership. The ambient spaces contain at most 256 words, so exhaustive comparisons are practical and their limits are explicit.

For the MacWilliams comparison, one implementation evaluates exact integer Krawtchouk sums. A separate coefficient-array calculation expands the relevant products, and direct enumeration supplies the dual weight distribution. Agreement is same-owner finite software evidence. It is not an independently reproduced experiment, a measured noise model, a physical law, a cryptographic security claim or an empirical confirmation of GMUT. The mathematical note explains the character-sum identity and the point at which empirical evidence would become necessary.
