## What the mathematics actually establishes

A polynomial of degree at most d is represented by d+1 coefficients. A sample at x supplies a linear equation whose row is the sequence 1, x, x squared, and subsequent powers up to d. A stack of such rows produces a Vandermonde-type design matrix. The runtime uses normalized rational numbers with BigInt numerators and denominators and deterministic row reduction. Rank, inconsistency, a particular solution, and a nullspace basis are returned as explicit parts of the result rather than concealed behind a single fitted curve.

If the constraints are consistent, every solution is the particular solution plus a linear combination of nullspace vectors. Setting free coordinates to zero produces a convenient particular solution, not evidence that those coordinates have been identified. A quantity represented by a linear functional is identified exactly when it annihilates every nullspace direction. This distinction is the phase's principal teaching surface: a whole coefficient vector can remain undetermined while a sample-node prediction, an integral, or another specific observable is determined.

For a simple quadratic example, the sole sample p(0)=3 fixes the constant term while leaving the linear and quadratic coefficients free. The family is p(x)=3+u x+v x squared. All members satisfy the observed constraint, but p(1)=3+u+v varies. The five browser models generalize this demonstration with different rational sample nodes, targets, and particular solutions. Their two displayed axes are coefficient coordinates, not probabilities, posterior uncertainty, measured error bars, or physical parameters established by observation.

Another distinction concerns symmetric integration. An odd free direction can integrate to zero on a symmetric interval even though its coefficient is not identified. Conversely, a derivative can remain free at a point whose value is constrained. The additional test portfolio includes these cases so that 'identified value' cannot silently become 'identified derivative' or 'identified model.'
