# Finite Evidence Transport v1

Use this guide for bounded movement or combination of finite evidence records.

## Method

1. Validate every input frame and mass record independently.
2. Declare the exact operator and its convention before execution.
3. For combination, retain conflict explicitly and reject a zero normalization denominator.
4. For conditioning, declare the conditioning subset and preserve the precondition record.
5. For extension or marginalization, declare the frame map and reject missing, duplicate, or out-of-range coordinates.
6. Preserve exact rational arithmetic, source identifiers, and rollback data.

## Acceptance

Accept only deterministic finite outputs with a complete frame map, exact totals, conflict disclosure, and a reversible provenance link. Retain all rejected inputs and failed conventions at zero completion credit.

## Boundaries

Transport is finite software algebra. It does not transport real-world trust, consent, identity, authority, ownership, evidence custody, legal status, cultural legitimacy, Maori authority, scientific confirmation, professional responsibility, or affected-party permission.
