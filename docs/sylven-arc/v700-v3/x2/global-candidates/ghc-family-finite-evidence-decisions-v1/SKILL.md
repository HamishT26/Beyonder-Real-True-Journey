# Finite Evidence Decisions v1

Use this guide only after a finite evidence record has passed frame and transform validation.

## Method

1. Name the chosen finite decision convention before computing.
2. For a pignistic summary, distribute each non-empty focal mass exactly across its declared members.
3. For a finite Choquet-style sum, declare the capacity, ordering convention, and tie handling.
4. Emit exact rational results and deterministic labels.
5. Keep mathematical ranking separate from an action recommendation.
6. Attach a decision-authority reservation to every result.

## Required refusal

Reject empty-set mass where the chosen convention cannot consume it, undeclared utilities, ambiguous tie rules, missing affected-party authority, or any request to convert the finite calculation directly into a professional, legal, cultural, medical, safety, employment, or governance decision.

## Boundaries

A finite decision summary is a synthetic calculation, not an authorized decision. It supplies no participant evidence, empirical validity, professional qualification, deployment readiness, legal effect, cultural legitimacy, Maori authority, identity status, or Stage 20 authority.
