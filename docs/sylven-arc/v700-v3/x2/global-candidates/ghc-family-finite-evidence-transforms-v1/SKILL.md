# Finite Evidence Transforms v1

Use this guide for exact transforms on an already validated finite evidence frame.

## Method

1. Require a valid finite-frame receipt before transformation.
2. Use exact integer or rational arithmetic across all subset enumerations.
3. Declare the transform direction: mass to belief, mass to plausibility, mass to commonality, or a supported inverse Mobius recovery.
4. Preserve mask ordering and the source-frame identifier.
5. Verify the transform with a bounded round trip where an inverse is declared.
6. Reject unsupported or ambiguous conventions rather than choosing one silently.

## Acceptance

An accepted result has the same declared frame, exact rational values, deterministic mask ordering, a named transform, and a falsifier such as failed round-trip equality. A rejection retains the malformed subject and reason.

## Boundaries

These transforms manipulate finite set functions. They do not establish a human mental state, probability calibration, physical observation, causal law, identity claim, scientific authority, professional advice, legal or cultural judgment, affected-party agreement, Maori authority, independent reproduction, or Stage 20 readiness.
