# Finite Evidence Boundaries v1

Use this guide whenever mathematical evidence-function language could be mistaken for real-world evidence or authority.

## Boundary classes

- `formal_only`: exact finite algebra or software structure.
- `represented`: a documented analogy without real observation.
- `open_gap`: missing empirical, participant, accessibility, calibration, or independent-review evidence.
- `exact_gate`: a protected action or claim requiring specific authority and evidence.

## Method

1. Identify every noun that could imply a person, observation, identity, right, decision, or authority.
2. Bind each claim to its actual finite artifact and source status.
3. Refuse conversion from formal mass or belief vocabulary into human belief measurement.
4. Keep observation and affected-user needs as open gaps when no real evidence exists.
5. Keep professional, legal, cultural, Maori-authority, identity-lifecycle, production, and deployment actions exact-gated.
6. Preserve the original request, rejection, and bounded recovery without erasure.

## Output

Return an explicit disposition, missing evidence or authority, rollback instruction, and nonclaim statement. Do not promote same-owner validation into independent reproduction, complete privacy or accessibility assurance, empirical science, consciousness, personhood, Theory-of-Everything proof, canon, or Stage 20 authority.
