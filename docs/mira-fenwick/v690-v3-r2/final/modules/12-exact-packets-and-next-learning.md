# Exact packets, blocked actions and next learning

The exact-packet ledger separates user authorization from implementation and terminal prerequisites. Hamish has already authorized the suitable concrete owner actions. At the x2 seal, forty-four of fifty exact actions were complete. The remaining six were baton composition, final content sealing, exact final commit/push, fresh equality, the one canonical and the one guarded existing-Auren message. Final authoring completes the file-backed part; the later repository and delivery events are recorded externally. A file should not pretend to contain a future acknowledgement.

Thirty blocked packets remain unexecuted. They cover real participant enrollment, regulated advice, live identity and key actions, public authority claims, Maori determinations, real asset allocation, unreviewed production/cloud actions, real autonomous purchases, credential export, source-text execution, archive deletion, sibling mutation, destructive Git, resending an accepted handoff, substitute task creation, model overrides, standby activation, unbounded tool loops, app replacement, security weakening, empirical GMUT confirmation, consciousness and identity claims, ASI certification, world-governance enforcement, personal-data intake, unlicensed game assets and Stage 20 declarations. Their absence is part of the evidence, not a deficit hidden by a pass count.

The next two practice recommendations are agent simulation experiment design and consent/recourse usability research. The first can define a small model-driven actor comparison with fixed tools, budgets, seeds, stop conditions and a scripted baseline. The second can ask whether a person can understand a withdrawal, see its effect, inspect retained history and obtain a meaningful remedy. Neither recommendation authorizes real participants or outside authority by itself.

Five next skill ideas are partial-observation simulation evaluation, model-driven NPC tool-budget review, consent-withdrawal usability review, recovery-trace comparability review and physics-observable specification editing. Five runner ideas are a matched-seed policy comparator, persistent-save reviewer, bounded NPC-planner replay, participant-free recourse scenario generator and scalar-model falsification matrix. These are proposals, not installed capabilities or already passed experiments.

An appropriate next software advance would separate the world-state reducer from a candidate planner, keep the planner's tool budget explicit and compare it with the current scripted policies. A planner that narrates success while violating the reducer's resource or consent rules should fail. A real-world study would need much more: legitimate participants, consent, safety review, a sound comparison design and appropriate statistical interpretation.

An appropriate GMUT advance would choose one physical specialization and one possible observable instead of expanding undefined symbols. Fix the action, normalization, units and parameter domain, identify what data could distinguish it from a baseline, and write down an outcome that would count against it. Keep theological or personal meaning separate from that empirical test. An appropriate Freed ID advance would preserve minimal disclosure, revocation, recovery, reliance policy and affected-party oversight without calling a synthetic flag a legal right.

The present work is intentionally revisable. A future owner may choose different focal points and better tools within current authority. It should retain this record rather than rewriting it into a success claim that the evidence does not support. NOT_READY_FOR_STAGE_20 remains the current conclusion.
