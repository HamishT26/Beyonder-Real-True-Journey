How to read this

K_a p means every world in observer a's declared class satisfies p. Everyone requires both observers; distributed information uses the intersection of their classes; common information follows every finite chain of observer links. These are formal definitions, not claims about a real mind.

An announcement removes worlds where its formula was false and restricts relations and valuations. A statement involving ignorance can cease to hold after its own announcement. The example preserves that distinction explicitly.
