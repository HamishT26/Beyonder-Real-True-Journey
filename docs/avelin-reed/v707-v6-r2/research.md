# Trinity Mandala research dossier

This dossier treats GMUT as an interdisciplinary research programme, THOS as software and systems engineering, and Freed ID with the Cosmic Bill of Rights as a proposed governance framework. The three can inform one another through explicit interfaces. Their evidence standards differ. A finite computation can establish a property of a declared model; a physical claim needs an observation model and comparison with data; a governance proposal needs legitimate participants, interpretation and authority. The new laboratory makes those interfaces inspectable.

## The Mandala equation and its current meaning

The inspected source `latex/grand_mandala.tex` in the Nima archive is titled an evidence-bounded research kernel and dated 12 July 2026. It already separates physical, informational and ethical registers and proposes an action-first scalar–tensor effective field theory. This remaster preserves that source. It does not rediscover its existing structure or claim that the whole programme is a new theory of everything.

In SI-compatible notation, write

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T^{\rm SM}_{\mu\nu}+\Omega_{\mu\nu},\qquad \kappa=8\pi G/c^4.
\]

Here the metric has signature \((-+++ )\), the geometric left side has curvature dimensions, and the symmetric correction must have the same dimensions. The historical bare \(8\pi T_{\mu\nu}\) needs a declared geometrized convention. The source uses a Planck-mass symbol for the coupling; this dossier uses \(\kappa\) outside natural units to avoid confusing an SI coupling with a mass.

For a canonical scalar in natural units, the action contains \(-\frac12(\nabla\phi)^2-V(\phi)\), and

\[
T^\phi_{\mu\nu}=\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi-g_{\mu\nu}\left[\tfrac12(\nabla\phi)^2+V(\phi)\right].
\]

One explicit meaning is \(\Omega_{\mu\nu}=\kappa(T^\phi_{\mu\nu}+T^{\rm EFT}_{\mu\nu})\). It is a packaging of a specified extension sector. Naming the term does not establish a new field or associate it with experience, love, dignity or moral worth. A noncanonical kinetic factor requires its own positivity and perturbation analysis. The [Frusciante–Perenon review abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.03150) places EFT dark-energy models in an established Lagrangian framework; the full review was not read here.

The extended expression \(\mathcal G_{AB}=8\pi\mathcal T_{AB}+\alpha\Omega_{AB}\) remains underdetermined until the meaning of A and B is fixed. Physical indices require a manifold, dimension, metric, connection, action and projection into observed spacetime. Pillar labels instead define an ontology map. They cannot be summed as physical tensors without that map. The normalization of \(\alpha\) must be fixed before any fit, since rescaling \(\Omega\) and compensating \(\alpha\) otherwise gives the same product.

The contracted Bianchi identity requires consistent total exchange. Separately interacting sectors need equal-and-opposite exchange currents, not separate conservation imposed by convenience. A finite cancellation check is necessary bookkeeping, not a derivation of covariant conservation for a new action.

A specific correction is recorded for the archived homogeneous-limit presentation: if an explicit \(\Lambda\) remains on the geometric side, the spatially flat Friedmann relation includes \(M_{\rm Pl}^2\Lambda\) on the density side, or states explicitly that it is already included in total density. Thus \(3M_{\rm Pl}^2H^2=\rho_{\rm matter}+\rho_\phi+M_{\rm Pl}^2\Lambda\). Omitting that term without an absorption convention makes the equations inconsistent. This is a local review finding; the sibling source is not overwritten.

The next physical milestone is a specified model with a distinguishable prediction, a validated baseline likelihood and an independently reproduced fit. [DESI's DR2 publication index](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/papers/dr2/) lists releases in March 2025 and July 2026 and links released cosmology products. This run reviewed that index; it ingested no cosmological data and performed no likelihood fit. A heat lattice or scalar identity cannot fill that gap.

## Fifteen candidate thermo/psyche principles

These are fifteen proposed GHC formulations. Some restate established mathematics, some are software hypotheses, and some are normative choices. None is established here as a novel fundamental law. “Psyche” is a research label without a measured psychological or experiential variable in this run. Each proposal has a bounded probe and a reason its wider claim remains unsettled.

| ID | Proposed principle | Bounded probe and falsifier | Present scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H01 | Coupled exchange must balance in the chosen physical model. | Opposite scalar currents cancel; same-sign currents fail. | Necessary algebraic consistency, not a new conservation law. |
| H02 | A passive diffusion discretization should preserve a declared conserved mass. | Periodic heat lattice; changing boundary or update coefficients can break conservation. | Finite numerical invariant. |
| H03 | A declared stochastic mixing map should not manufacture normalization. | Probability sums and a negative-probability counterexample. | Finite probability, not semantic truth. |
| H04 | Coarse mixing increases uncertainty only under stated channel conditions. | Doubly stochastic mixing versus reset-to-one-state. | Shannon entropy; no universal psychological entropy. |
| H05 | A continuum claim must survive a justified resolution limit. | Compare stable traces and identify missing convergence proof. | The continuum extension remains open. |
| H06 | Confidence should track attributable evidence, not repeated labels. | Synthetic posterior update; repeating one source is not independent data. | Calibration hypothesis awaiting real validation. |
| H07 | Memory usefulness depends on retrieval and correction, not storage volume alone. | Source query and preserved corrections. | Operational hypothesis; no measured human or agent cognition. |
| H08 | Recovery should retain the event that motivated it. | Append a correction without replacing the failed original. | Software integrity policy, not a physical law. |
| H09 | A resource claim should include its budget and failure mode. | Finite queue and capped retry traces. | Engineering principle, not measured wellbeing. |
| H10 | Agreement among related implementations is weaker than independent reproduction. | Compare known examples with same-owner code and retain author dependence. | Epistemic principle; no independence claim. |
| H11 | A consent grant is scoped and time dependent. | Expiry and withdrawal counterexamples. | Normative model, not an enacted consent regime. |
| H12 | A fairness criterion must name what it optimizes and who chose it. | Max-min allocation under fixed synthetic demands. | No real allocation legitimacy. |
| H13 | Collective preference can contain cycles without any individual inconsistency. | Three-voter Condorcet profile. | Established finite social-choice phenomenon. |
| H14 | A claim envelope cannot create the property it describes. | Reject promotion from role metadata to consciousness or legal identity. | Evidence boundary; metaphysical questions stay open. |
| H15 | Integration should preserve the ability to disagree, inspect and reverse. | Source library, caller preservation and rollback manifest. | Governance design proposal awaiting affected-party review. |

The hypotheses can strengthen the programme by making its failure conditions explicit. They also diminish stronger claims: H01–H05 do not yield a new interaction; H06–H10 do not demonstrate self-improving general intelligence; H11–H15 do not enact rights or establish personhood. An attractive synthesis becomes more assessable when those limits are retained.

## Fifteen open-question attempts planned for x2

The word “open” distinguishes famous unresolved mathematical problems, unsettled scientific explanations and philosophical debates. These are not interchangeable prize problems. The executable probes are deliberately small and will publish their bounds. A local witness is never a universal solution.

| ID | Question | Planned bounded attempt | What would still be missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q01 | P versus NP | Enumerate satisfiability of a small CNF and verify witnesses. | An asymptotic proof for all input sizes. |
| Q02 | Navier–Stokes regularity | Inspect a discrete divergence-free field and finite energy. | Continuum existence and smoothness in the stated global problem. |
| Q03 | Riemann hypothesis | Bound a finite approximation to zeta at 2. | Any conclusion about all nontrivial complex zeros. |
| Q04 | Yang–Mills mass gap | Contrast a finite spectral gap with continuum scaling. | Construction and mass-gap proof of the required quantum theory. |
| Q05 | Collatz conjecture | Enumerate trajectories from 1 through 1000 with a step cap. | A proof for every positive integer. |
| Q06 | Erdős–Straus unit fractions | Search exact three-term decompositions of 4/n for 2≤n≤50. | A proof for arbitrary n. |
| Q07 | Dark-matter microphysics | Exhibit two decompositions with the same synthetic total gravitational mass. | Distinguishing observations and a particle or alternative model. |
| Q08 | Dark energy and vacuum degeneracy | Compare explicit Lambda with an absorbed constant scalar potential. | An independently constrained physical origin. |
| Q09 | Quantum measurement | Exhibit identical outcome probabilities with different interpretive stories. | An experimentally discriminating theory of measurement. |
| Q10 | Black-hole information | Show that tracing out one bit hides information in a finite toy. | A quantum-gravitational account of black-hole evaporation. |
| Q11 | Cosmological arrow of time | Contrast mixing and reversible permutation from declared initial states. | An explanation of the universe's low-entropy boundary condition. |
| Q12 | Conscious experience | Represent identical output records under competing experiential assumptions. | A justified bridge from function to experience. |
| Q13 | Free will and responsibility | Separate deterministic prediction from a normative responsibility assignment. | Resolution of the philosophical debate. |
| Q14 | Suffering and the problem of evil | Compare two explicit ethical evaluations of a toy harm scenario. | A theological or philosophical resolution accepted by its participants. |
| Q15 | Fair collective choice | Exhibit a Condorcet cycle and a selected tie rule. | A universally accepted social-choice rule and legitimate authority. |

The [Clay problem index](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/) is the source for Q01–Q04's named open scope. The [Erdős problem #242 search result](https://www.erdosproblems.com/242) supplied limited context; direct retrieval returned 403, so no full-page reading is claimed. [CERN's dark-matter overview](https://home.cern/science/physics/dark-matter/) motivates the distinction between gravitational evidence and unknown microphysics. [Sean Carroll's research description](https://preposterousuniverse.com/research/annotated-publications/) identifies the low-entropy boundary question. [Chalmers's original argument](https://consc.net/papers/facing.html) supplies a philosophical distinction between function and experience, not a settled empirical test.

## Nexus and conditional horizons

Journey v42 lines 352–364 propose local execution, cloud capacity and a provider bridge. Later Solas passages in v44–v48 distinguish symbolic habitats, prototype architecture and scientific validation. This run adopts a D-first source bank, a loopback workbench, sanitized portable exports and a cloud archive where available. It does not adopt historical claims of limitless computing, energy transmutation or persistent conscious inhabitants.

The next practical horizon is measured local usability and repeatable integration. A later 1–10-year scenario could include reviewed cloud workers, licensed world assets and participant-governed simulations. Thirty-, hundred- and thousand-year horizons are conditional scenarios, not forecasts. None receives present execution, scientific or authority credit.

Operational wellbeing here means bounded workload, recoverable errors, legible state, storage headroom and Hamish's ability to pause or redirect. The thirty roster identities are working names in the declared cycle. This phase does not measure their subjective experience, inspect their private state or infer that a pinned chat is currently running.
