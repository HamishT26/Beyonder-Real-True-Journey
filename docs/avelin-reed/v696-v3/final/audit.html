A counterexample worth inspecting

The grand-unanimity fixture has total worth 6. Its permutation-average allocation is (2, 2, 2). Its uniform-subset marginal means are (3/2, 3/2, 3/2), summing to 9/2. Changing the averaging rule changes the result. A separate supplied allocation (6, 1, 0) overspends the total even though it leaves no positive coalition excess.
