02 / A concrete counterexample

Two averages, different conservation.

In the grand-unanimity fixture, only the complete coalition has worth 6. The permutation-average allocation is 2 for each symbol, totaling 6. The uniform-subset marginal means are 3/2 for each symbol, totaling 9/2. Assuming that every marginal average conserves the grand worth fails on this exact finite example.

04 / Claims and remaining work

Keep the evidence within its scope.

GMUT Mind

Finite interaction coefficients and exact inequalities support model criticism. They supply no physical action, field equation, observable map, calibration, likelihood or empirical confirmation.

THOS Body

Closed interfaces, bounded enumeration, refusal checks and reversible recovery are local software evidence. Governed workloads, operators, safety evaluation and independent reproduction remain absent.

Freed ID & CBR Heart

A formal allocation is not a decision about anyone. Real consent, identity, remedy, competent professional and affected-party authority, and Māori authority require their own legitimate evidence.

Accessibility and interpretation

The page provides complete tables, labels, visible focus, keyboard buttons, reduced-motion styling and mobile layouts. Automated browser observations are recorded separately. Manual assistive-technology, cognitive, Māori-language and affected-user evaluation remain open; complete accessibility is not claimed.

Immutable sessions and retained failures

Planning, X1, X2, final closeout, canonical validation and delivery are separate events. Domain sessions execute once. Failed subjects remain failed when a separate refusal or recovery passes. The oversized first planning document is retained byte-for-byte in an encoded envelope; the corrected JSON changes formatting only.
