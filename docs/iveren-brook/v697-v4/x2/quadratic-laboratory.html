Evidence and interpretation
Stationarity is a separate question
The equation Hx = −g describes stationary points. An inconsistent system has none; a singular consistent system has free directions. Neither a convenient particular solution nor a zero gradient automatically establishes a minimum.
Box witnesses are bounded
Corner and edge candidates, together with an admitted stationary representative, witness the least value on the declared two-variable box. A finite candidate list does not enumerate an entire continuous minimizer set.
Certificates retain their conditions
A convex certificate requires positive semidefiniteness, feasibility and the correct boundary-gradient signs. A nonconvex box minimum can still exist while this sufficient certificate is withheld.
The model rows and minima were compared against a separately authored planning Fraction oracle. These are same-owner software checks, not independent reproduction or empirical model validation.
