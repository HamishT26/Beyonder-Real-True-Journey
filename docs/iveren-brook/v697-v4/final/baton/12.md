## 12. Final packet, manifests and canonical admission

The final overview develops the domain, evidence and continuation in three substantial sections. This complete baton is also split into thirteen source-bound modules with an index. The final packet includes lineage, source intake references, proposal/result joins, workload reconciliation, accounting, negative register, operational failures, reflection decisions, authority state, open gates, environment and checklist records. Artifact ceilings are checked against actual selected files; a ceiling is not a quota.

Six manifest domains bind planning, x1, x2, the deck, the final delta and the whole current owner phase. The final-delta and whole-phase manifests explicitly exclude themselves and each other to avoid a hash cycle. Their exact bytes are then covered by the final Git commit and external canonical. A separate content seal binds selected substantive artifacts. The canonical reads only this owner's current phase content plus exact commit metadata. It does not scan or execute unchanged prior lifecycle content, source tranches, local domain code, model generators or installed capability tests.

Canonical admission requires the exact final head, a clean index and worktree, a pushed branch, fresh local/upstream/tracking/live equality, exact own ancestry, bounded blobs and an exclusive external attempt latch. It runs once. An unsuccessful canonical is retained, not overwritten or silently retried. A successful canonical is never replayed. Any later correction must follow a specifically authorized additive dependency path. Repository preparation, canonical success, route resolution and delivery acknowledgement remain four separate states.
