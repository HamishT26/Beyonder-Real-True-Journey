\section{Exact interval domain}
Let \(A=[a_-,a_+]\), \(B=[b_-,b_+]\), with rational, finite, ordered
endpoints. A point interval is permitted. Inputs are canonical reduced strings
with numerator magnitude and denominator at most one million. Intermediate
numerators and denominators are bounded to 512 bits, and iteration to eight
steps. These are software resource bounds, not physical validity bounds.
\begin{align}
 A+B&=[a_-+b_-,a_++b_+],\\
 A-B&=[a_--b_+,a_+-b_-],\\
 AB&=[\min P,\max P],\quad
 P=\{a_-b_-,a_-b_+,a_+b_-,a_+b_+\}.
\end{align}
When \(0\notin B\), \(B^{-1}=[1/b_+,1/b_-]\) and division uses
\(AB^{-1}\). Otherwise division is refused, including endpoint zero.
For a single variable, \(A^2\) has lower bound zero if \(0\in A\); otherwise
its lower bound is the smaller endpoint square. Its upper bound is the larger
endpoint square. For \(q(x)=c_2x^2+c_1x+c_0\), evaluate both endpoints and
the stationary point \(-c_1/(2c_2)\) when it exists inside the interval.
