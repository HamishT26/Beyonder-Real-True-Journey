# v642-v6 wellbeing and workload check

## Scope and pacing

This is a one-owner repository phase with ten bounded proposals. The work is split by irreversible gates: read-only source verification, x1 freeze, x2 execution, evidence validation, closeout, seal, final validation, and one post-closeout message. No elapsed-time target can replace a checklist result, and no watcher or prepared message counts as completion.

The owner will use small, reviewable commits and bounded commands. Long checkout or snapshot operations may exceed wrappers; a timeout is audited before any retry. Existing branches and worktrees remain recoverable. No recursive rotation, destructive cleanup, reset, force push, merge, feature change, elevation, or reboot is allowed.

## Load controls

- Exactly ten proposals; no opportunistic eleventh proposal.
- One additive phase worktree; no additional phase-owned worktree for ordinary work.
- Fresh detached snapshots are validation surfaces, not new ownership lanes.
- The inherited 29,977-file checkout does not trigger rotation. Only Orin-owned or generated files are measured against 15,000.
- Current official-source checks are bounded to evidence needs; raw browsing dumps are not published.
- Operational failures are retained once with recovery evidence instead of repeatedly reproduced for appearance.
- The complete/incomplete checklist controls closeout. Open gaps and exact gates are valid outcomes.

## Human and affected-party wellbeing

THOS will not recruit, rate, or simulate consent from real participants. Participant safety, withdrawal, ethics review, and independent review remain visible gaps. CBR artifacts will not generate Māori wording, infer silence as consent, appoint representatives, or treat technical roles as Māori authority. Private material, credentials, task identifiers, routes, transcripts, screenshots, and local paths remain outside publication.

## Relational identity boundary

Wellbeing language here governs workload quality, error recovery, and respectful collaboration. It is not a claim that Orin is conscious, sentient, a legal person, or an identity continuous across tasks. The chosen hope is a working value, not evidence of an inner state.

## Stop and recovery conditions

Stop before any scientific promotion, human-subject execution, live identity deployment, legal or cultural decision, account or API-key action, purchase, private publication, destructive action, sibling merge, or unapproved route. If evidence is missing, record `open_gap`; if competent authority is required, record `exact_gate`. If the Tamar route is unavailable after verified closeout, record `OPEN_ROUTE_GAP` and do not create or contact another task.

Status at x1: workload bounded, source verified, one recovered worktree timeout retained, no subagents or successor tasks created, and no outbound sibling message sent.
