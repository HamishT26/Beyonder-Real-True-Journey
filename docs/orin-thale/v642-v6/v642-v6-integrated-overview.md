## Cross-pillar evidence integrity

Four proposals operate across all three pillars. The first creates a bidirectional trace between frozen requirements and evidence. It rejects both an unsupported requirement and an orphan artifact that lacks a preregistered acceptance role. This matters because artifact count is not evidence quality: a directory can grow while the central claim remains unsupported. The trace also refuses circular evidence and prevents a post-freeze artifact from silently changing the acceptance rule it is supposed to satisfy.

The reproduction proposal uses step ablation. A clean recipe is not assumed to be sufficient merely because two same-owner snapshots match. Removing a required step should produce the preregistered failure; if it does not, the recipe may contain redundancy or hidden state. Conversely, a cache or environment dependency that changes output without being declared must become a retained negative. Even strong parity remains same-owner internal repeatability, not independent-team scientific reproduction.

The thermo-psyche proposal adds an aggregation-level barrier. Earlier phases separated thermodynamic from Shannon entropy, classified measurement scales, and constrained causal language. v642-v6 asks whether an individual-level claim is being inferred from group statistics, whether a group law is being inferred from individual associations, or whether subgroup composition hides a Simpson reversal. An explicit level bridge is required. Without one, an analogy remains a labeled analogy and cannot become a fundamental law or consciousness claim.

The Stage 20 proposal prevents evidence-class conversion. Local tests remain engineering evidence. Synthetic fixtures remain synthetic representation. A protocol remains a protocol. Same-owner snapshots remain same-owner snapshots. None can be relabeled as empirical confirmation, legal interpretation, cultural ratification, production assurance, or independent reproduction. This differs from simply assigning a low score: the class conversion itself is invalid. A large local score cannot purchase missing authority.
