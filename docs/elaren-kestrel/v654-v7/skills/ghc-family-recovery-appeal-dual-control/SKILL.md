1. Separate requester, first reviewer, independent reviewer, and escalation authority.
2. Record evidence quality, timeout, conflict, notification, correction, and remedy paths.
3. Reject unilateral completion, missing appeal, or silent evidence substitution.
4. Keep real identity recovery, participant outcomes, legal decisions, and professional validation outside the fixture.

Use `ghc_family_recovery_appeal_dual_control.py`. Retain both the failed and corrected workflow witnesses.
