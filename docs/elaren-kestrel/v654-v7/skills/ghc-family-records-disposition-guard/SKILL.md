# Records Disposition Guard

1. Identify the record class, source authority, retention trigger, minimum period, hold, and proposed action.
2. Require authorization provenance, approval, audit metadata, and a rollback or suspension path.
3. Reject destruction, transfer, or erasure claims when authority, evidence, or verification is absent.
4. Perform no real disposal, key destruction, legal interpretation, or public-records decision.

Use `ghc_family_records_disposition_guard.py` for synthetic fixtures. Archives guidance informs obligations but does not authorize this phase.
