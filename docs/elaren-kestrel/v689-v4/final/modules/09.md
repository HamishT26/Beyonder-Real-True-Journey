# Module 09: Packages and reproducibility

Three direct packages were installed in an isolated D-first environment from four exact hash-locked wheels: bitarray 3.11.0, portion 2.6.2, more-itertools 11.1.0, and the sortedcontainers 2.4.0 dependency. Two immutable planning hashes were transcription errors and remain failed evidence; exact filename metadata and downloaded bytes corrected them before installation. Three accepting smokes and three adverse boundaries passed only as a dependency-corrected composite. The audit was point-in-time and found no known vulnerability rows. It is not exhaustive supply-chain assurance. Hash agreement establishes byte correspondence, not semantic correctness, authorship, safety, consent, legitimacy, or future vulnerability freedom.
