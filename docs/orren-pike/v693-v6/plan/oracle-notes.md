# Expected-value authoring

The frozen expectations were authored with exact rational reference arithmetic before the production runner existed. They are definitions, not successful operation witnesses. The later runtime uses a separately written BigInt fraction implementation. Agreement remains same-owner evidence and is not independent reproduction.

For the unequal-escape fixture, states zero and one are grouped. Their probabilities into that same block are one half and one quarter, respectively. The complementary escape probabilities are one half and three quarters. The proposed representative quotient therefore fails the block-sum criterion. Starting from an equal mixture gives projected next probabilities three eighths and five eighths; the representative quotient instead yields one half and one half.

In the cascade, the initial partition groups states zero with one and two with three. The second block first splits because states two and three send all mass to different original blocks. That distinction then separates states zero and one on the second round. The result is the discrete partition. No merge across the initial blocks is permitted.

The stationary fixture starts with probabilities one sixth, one sixth, one third and one third. Its block projection is one third and two thirds; both the original and exact quotient distributions are stationary. These literal identities do not measure any physical transition rate, participant result or deployed service.

A one-step equality for a single initial distribution is not a proof of strong lumpability. The contract reports that narrow equality separately from the all-states block criterion. A finite-horizon comparison carries its horizon and does not claim an unbounded theorem by itself.
