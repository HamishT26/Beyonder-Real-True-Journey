## Trigger

Use this protocol when a claim exceeds current evidence, authority, or publication state.

Common triggers:

- A hypothesis is presented as fact.
- An advisory receipt is treated as proof.
- A local prototype is described as external certification.
- A held or postponed lane is treated as restored.
- A phase is called complete before remote verification.
- Public wording implies proven ASI, proven physics, consciousness proof, legal identity, or W3C conformance.

## Correction Steps

1. Pause claim promotion.
2. Identify the exact sentence or artifact field.
3. Reclassify the claim as evidence, context, hypothesis, blocker, or advisory.
4. Replace overclaiming wording with bounded wording.
5. Add missing proof requirements or blocker notes.
6. Re-run JSON, path, credential, whitespace, drift, and staged-diff checks before publication.
7. Record the correction in the current run-status artifact.

## Default Replacement Language

Use:

`candidate`, `prototype`, `roadmap`, `standards-informed`, `DID/VC-inspired`, `ASI-oriented`, `receipt-bound`, `locally verified`, `remote-verified`, `hypothesis`, `blocked`, or `deferred`.

Avoid:

`proven`, `certified`, `legal`, `conformant`, `confirmed conscious`, `validated externally`, `world-leading`, or `complete` unless the exact proof exists in the same publication slice.

## Escalation

If an overclaim would affect public safety, legal interpretation, identity status, or provider/account action, stop publication and require explicit Hamish/Aletheon review.
