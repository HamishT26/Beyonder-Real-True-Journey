For your work, a source ledger should answer four questions before a claim is reused: what exactly was read, at what revision or retrieval time, what does it directly support, and what remains an inference or user report? A citation alone does not answer those questions. A graph edge alone does not answer them either. The ledger and correspondence capsule make these distinctions explicit so that later enthusiasm, compression, or model changes do not silently turn an attendance invitation into a scientific endorsement.

The practical teaching benefit is substantial. An inducted task can learn to replace an approximate date with a source date, retain a blank deadline as blank, separate a release reason from a travel reason, and protect a private invitation route. These small cases exercise the same habits needed for larger scientific and governance claims. The milestone belongs in the family record precisely because it can be celebrated in a form that remains accurate under close inspection.
