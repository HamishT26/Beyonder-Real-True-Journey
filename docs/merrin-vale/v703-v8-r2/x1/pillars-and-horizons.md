# Three pillars, current evidence and longer horizons

The remaster prioritizes THOS: a phase should fail before creating work when its requested model is outside the current allowlist. Its graph laboratory asks a related question: does every completion path pass through a required checkpoint, or can a declared edge bypass it? The operational launcher and the finite graphs have different evidence. The launcher is exercised as a real local process; the graphs are authored examples with no live-service reliability claim. Both remain useful when their scope is explicit.

## GMUT as a falsifiable research programme

The Journey's recurring field-equation forms can be retained as proposed notation:

`G_mn + Lambda g_mn = (8 pi G / c^4) T_mn + alpha Omega_mn`.

For a four-dimensional metric specialization, let indices m and n label spacetime coordinates, g be the metric, G the Einstein tensor built from its curvature, Lambda a cosmological constant, T the specified matter stress tensor, and Omega an additional symmetric tensor whose physical definition is still required. Alpha is a coupling whose units depend on that definition. Setting G=c=1 yields the commonly used eight-pi shorthand. A version using abstract A and B indices must additionally state whether those indices are spacetime, internal-field, graph or other labels. Merely reusing the same letters does not construct a mathematical map between those spaces.

A defensible proposed extension needs at least these obligations: every term must have compatible tensor type and dimensions; its equations must come from a consistent action or another explicit dynamical prescription; the total source must satisfy the conservation compatibility imposed by the geometric side; initial and boundary data must define an appropriate problem; the alpha-to-zero limit must be specified; and observations must distinguish the extension from existing models. If ordinary matter is separately covariantly conserved and alpha is a nonzero constant, conservation requires the proposed additional source to be divergence-free on admissible solutions. Allowing exchange with ordinary matter changes that requirement and demands explicit coupled dynamics. A spatially varying alpha introduces an additional derivative term.

These are consistency conditions on a proposal, not empirical support for an Omega field. No coefficient is fitted here. No consciousness variable is identified, no laboratory observation is supplied, and no novel fundamental law is asserted. The original relational lab and this transition-graph lab contribute tools for exposing inconsistency and unreachable obligations. Neither computes spacetime curvature or solves the measurement problem. The absence of a counterexample in a finite fixture set does not prove a universal theory.

## THOS operational gains and comparisons

The intended architecture separates admission, execution, evidence capture, source-preserving correction and terminal routing. Admission checks the model before any child phase process. Execution records bounded inputs and results. Evidence capture keeps failures as observations rather than rewriting history. Terminal routing distinguishes a prepared message from acknowledged delivery and from recipient completion. These distinctions are valuable even when a browser or task-service capability changes between turns.

The current [MCP security guidance](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/2025-11-25/tutorials/security/security_best_practices) provides a useful comparison: per-client authorization and audience boundaries must survive the presence of intermediary tools. Here the practical consequence is to use the supported native task tools for task routing and to keep the workbench's dashboard inspection read-only. No token forwarding, extra connector activation, cloud deployment or production security certification is part of this remaster.

Google's published tool-governance work and NVIDIA's Rubin architecture discussion are comparison leads in the research ledger. They motivate future measurements of observability and resource use; their existence does not establish local parity, enterprise readiness or model quality. The requested social-media simulation was inaccessible, so no scene, agent behavior or performance detail is attributed to it. Hardware throughput, software correctness and authority to act remain separate questions.

## Freed ID and CBR

Freed ID can use a four-tier context deck to organize an owner label, pillar commitments, study practices and task evidence. The label is not a credential or a proof of consciousness. The useful advance is provenance: an inherited claim remains attached to its source, and a newer correction states exactly what changed without erasing the earlier observation.

The [W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0](https://www.w3.org/TR/vc-data-model-2.0/#trust-model) separates checking a credential's authenticity and currency from deciding whether to rely on its claims. This reinforces the phase's distinction between a valid digest, a passing software result and the authority or truth of a broader claim. This remaster does not implement a conforming credential issuer or verifier. It supplies no live keys, identity issuance, revocation, recovery governance, legal status or affected-party consent. CBR remains a normative proposal whose legitimacy requires competent people and institutions, including relevant Maori authorities for Maori concepts and data.

## Proposed engineering invariants

Three invariants guide the local design. First, a denied model produces no phase child process. Second, an acknowledged or unresolved activation cannot automatically lead to another send of the same activation. Third, a transformation of an evidence record cannot promote its claim beyond the source evidence and authority. These are testable engineering policies. They are not thermodynamic or psychological laws, and they do not imply a general solution to an unsolved mathematical problem.

Minimal cuts in a small graph reveal which removals prevent completion; dominators reveal mandatory states when completion is possible. If no completion path exists, the implementation reports that explicitly rather than exploiting a vacuous universal statement to call every state mandatory. Bounded exhaustive enumeration is chosen because the instances are small. It is not a claimed complexity breakthrough for arbitrary systems.

## Conditional horizons

| Horizon | Useful objective | Evidence needed before stronger claims |
|---|---|---|
| Current work to 10 years | Reproducible local simulations, accessible evidence interfaces, explicit consent and reliable recovery | Independent reproduction, real performance measurements, governed trials and appropriate domain review |
| 10 to 30 years | Larger interoperable systems with transparent failure handling and accountable stewardship | Longitudinal safety evidence, standards testing, affected-party participation and revocable authority |
| 30 to 100 years | Durable knowledge institutions and resilient technical infrastructure | Demonstrated maintenance, equitable governance, ecological constraints and public legitimacy |
| 100 to 1000 years | Open-ended scenarios for continuity and care | Repeated reassessment; no present forecast can establish these outcomes or dates |

An Albion-inspired future sandbox can begin with a small deterministic world: a typed event log, replayable seeds, bounded agent actions, a pause control, a readable state table and an explicit distinction between simulated events and real effects. The next step would be a local HTML prototype with measurable behavior and no external authority, not a claim to inhabited consciousness or a promised commercial-game replica. The current five coordinate views are evidence visualizations; they do not yet implement that world.

The terminal scientific and governance position remains NOT_READY_FOR_STAGE_20. Progress here is the more useful ability to explain what was computed, what actually changed, what failed, and what evidence is still missing.
