# 06 — Quotient paths, information change and feature covers in x2

X2 compares lost and gained distinctions, membership gains under refinement, existential quotient edges, concrete path realizations, bounded observation languages, spurious words, minimum-cardinality feature covers, minimum-cost covers and evidence/authority reservations. Lost distinctions are not assumed to imply a pure coarsening: both directions of change are reported. Refining observations can enlarge the lower approximation and reduce the upper approximation under the declared finite contract, while making no empirical sensor-quality claim.

A quotient edge records an existential pair of concrete states. Two adjacent quotient edges may use different intermediate representatives and fail to compose into one path. The retained four-state example has cells A={s0}, B={s1,s2}, C={s3} and transitions s0 to s1, s1 to itself, s2 to s3 and s3 to itself. The abstract word A,B,C has both local edges but no concrete realization. The observed frontiers are {s0}, {s1}, then empty. Production propagates realizable frontiers and keeps a lexicographically first witness when one exists; the specification independently enumerates complete paths.

Observation languages have exactly the declared horizon, not an unbounded temporal interpretation. Quotient words are compared with concrete observation words to count spurious words and retain a first witness. Feature-cover procedures distinguish specified unordered goal pairs using supplied feature partitions. Dynamic programming preserves all minimum ties and is compared with exhaustive subset enumeration. Infeasible goals, empty goal portfolios and optima above the declared model budget remain explicit. Integer costs are not money or procurement permission. Providing all evidence-role labels still leaves external verification false; authority strings never grant authority.
