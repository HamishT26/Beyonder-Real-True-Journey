# Certain, possible and boundary membership under declared observations

This guide curates 5 complementary local units. Their source guides, exact original callers and retained observations remain preserved; selecting this group does not retire them.

## or_lower

Return the union of complete observation cells contained in the target set.

## or_upper

Return the union of observation cells meeting the target set, without interpreting possibility as probability.

## or_boundary

Keep possible-but-not-certain states explicit; an empty target and the whole universe have no boundary.

## or_discernibility

Enumerate unordered state pairs with different declared observations, without a privacy guarantee.

## or_refinement_gain

Compare lower and upper approximations before and after the common refinement with a second observation.
