# Partition shape, refinement, lattice bounds and partial block maps

This guide curates 5 complementary local units. Their source guides, exact original callers and retained observations remain preserved; selecting this group does not retire them.

## or_profile

Validate a complete finite supplied-state observation record without inferring measured sensors.

## or_refines

Compare equivalence relations; a finer left partition must never identify a pair separated on the right.

## or_common_refinement

Intersect the two indiscernibility relations and preserve every resulting nonempty cell.

## or_common_coarsening

Join equivalence relations through their transitive closure; direct pair union alone is insufficient.

## or_block_map

Map a left cell to one right cell only when every member agrees; keep split cells unresolved.
