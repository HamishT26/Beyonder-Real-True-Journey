[
G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
= 8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}

]
Where:
            * (G_{\mu\nu}) is the Einstein tensor.
            * (\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}) is the cosmological constant term.
            * (T_{\mu\nu}) is ordinary matter-energy.
            * (\Omega_{\mu\nu}) is the proposed Ω/Ψ field stress-energy or effective source tensor.
            * (\alpha) is a coupling coefficient, constrained to be very small unless evidence permits otherwise.
This is the equation family your older GMUT documents repeatedly return to, including the v16 ψ-field synthesis and the Master Δ-Table.

v463 experimental constraints
GMUT’s Ω/Ψ term must survive the following constraints.
GR / gravitational-wave speed
Any Ω coupling must not cause large deviations in gravitational wave propagation. GW170817 was observed in both gravitational waves and light; LIGO says the gamma-ray flash arrived about two seconds after the gravitational waves from a source around 130 million light-years away. (LIGO Lab | Caltech) A related analysis says the near-simultaneous GW170817/GRB170817A detection constrained gravitational-wave speed to match gamma rays to about one part in (10^{15}). (arXiv)
So v463 should impose:
[
|c_g/c - 1| \lesssim 10^{-15}
]
for any Ω modification affecting gravitational wave speed.
CMB / ΛCDM baseline
Planck 2018 found good consistency with the standard six-parameter flat (\Lambda)CDM cosmology. (arXiv) Therefore GMUT must reproduce ΛCDM-like behavior in the early universe and at CMB precision unless it proposes a measurable, constrained deviation.
Standard Model domain
CERN’s Standard Model page says the model includes electromagnetic, strong, and weak interactions and all their carrier particles, but not gravity; it is incomplete but still extremely successful in its tested domain. (CERN) Thus GMUT’s couplings to ordinary matter must remain tiny enough that collider, QED, and particle-data constraints are not violated.
Dark energy dynamics
DESI hints about evolving dark energy can be used as a motivating target, not as proof. v463 should define a (w(z)) scan for the Ψ field:
[
w_\Psi(z)
\frac{\frac{1}{2}\dot{\Psi}^2 - V(\Psi)}
{\frac{1}{2}\dot{\Psi}^2 + V(\Psi)}
]
and compare it with ΛCDM’s (w=-1).
