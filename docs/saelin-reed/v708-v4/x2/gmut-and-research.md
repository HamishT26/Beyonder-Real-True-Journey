# GMUT as a typed research programme

The Mind contribution in this phase is a more explicit mathematical contract. The journey's field-equation notation is treated as a proposed research language. It does not yet establish a new fundamental interaction, a theory of everything, a theory of consciousness, or a theological conclusion. Software checks can reject inconsistent proposals and make a future empirical programme clearer; they cannot supply missing observations.

The four-dimensional starting point is

\[
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T^{(m)}_{\mu\nu}+\alpha\Omega_{\mu\nu},\qquad \kappa=8\pi G/c^4.
\]

Specify the metric signature as minus-plus-plus-plus, a four-dimensional Lorentzian manifold, a torsion-free Levi-Civita connection, and physical stress-energy on the right. Curvature and the cosmological term have dimensions of inverse length squared. Physical stress-energy needs its coupling; writing just 8πT requires an explicit geometrized-unit convention. The coefficient α and Ω must be assigned compatible dimensions as a pair. An unspecified “psyche” label cannot substitute for a tensor with an operational definition. The historical capital-index expression 𝒢_AB = 8π𝒯_AB + αΩ_AB remains a template until its index domain, geometry, units and matter content are defined.

For a concrete illustration, take natural units c = ℏ = 1 and a canonical real scalar field. Let

\[
S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{R-2\Lambda}{2\kappa}+\alpha\left(-\frac12g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi-V(\phi)\right)\right]+S_m.
\]

Define Ω_μν = κT^(φ)_μν, with T^(φ)_μν = ∂_μφ∂_νφ − g_μν[(∂φ)²/2 + V(φ)]. This is an illustrative standard scalar-matter extension, not a new derivation of the Journey's metaphysical claims. In these units [κ]=L², [T]=L⁻⁴, [φ]=L⁻¹ and [V]=L⁻⁴, giving [Ω]=L⁻². Positive α retains the canonical scalar kinetic sign in this elementary model; dimensional agreement alone says nothing about stability, boundary conditions, higher-derivative extensions or observational acceptability.

The contracted Bianchi identity requires conservation of the total source. Separate conservation of both sectors is an additional condition, not something to infer when energy exchange is allowed. Our finite divergence fixture deliberately admits [1,0,0,0] plus [−1,0,0,0] as a conserved total while each component is nonzero. This is algebraic bookkeeping; it is not a curved-spacetime field solution. At α=0 the extra source decouples. A constant scalar with V=0 also has zero stress in this example. A nonzero constant potential instead shifts the effective cosmological term. These limiting cases are useful rejection criteria for later implementations. The geometric and action framework follows [Carroll's general-relativity lecture notes](https://ned.ipac.caltech.edu/level5/March01/Carroll3/Carroll4.html).

A future empirical programme needs a definite action, well-posed equations, parameter priors, observable predictions, a reference model, a dataset with independent provenance, and a held-out comparison. It must state what would count against the proposal. Fitting finite points or passing a source-integrity gate is insufficient. The finite-fit probe in this phase shows that n and n+n(n−1)(n−2) agree at 0,1,2; adding the hypothetical datum (3,3) separates those functions but still says nothing about an unobserved latent label.

## Fifteen open questions and bounded attempts

The executable research-probes.cjs file records a distinct finite computation or counterexample for each of fifteen questions. The full questions are not solved. P versus NP, Navier–Stokes regularity and Yang–Mills mass gap are represented among the [Clay Millennium problems](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/). A finite SAT table, decaying linear mode or positive finite matrix gap cannot resolve their general statements. The zeta-series calibration does not establish anything about the nontrivial zeros described in [NIST DLMF's zeta-zero section](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10). Prime enumeration illustrates the gap between finite witnesses and infinitude; [Oxford's discussion of additive and multiplicative structures](https://www.maths.ox.ac.uk/node/32246) explains the twin-prime context.

The remaining probes cover Collatz, Goldbach, quantum gravity, dark matter, dark energy, quantum measurement, the arrow of time, consciousness, free will and plural moral objectives. Each result contains its stopping boundary and a statement of what does not follow. The metaphysical and ethical questions are contested philosophical questions rather than interchangeable mathematical conjectures. No technical credential, medical recommendation, political mandate or spiritual authority follows from studying them here.

## Journey continuity and long horizons

The v40–v52 selections contribute continuity themes: an integrated local/cloud bank, transparent assistance, creative simulation, retained memory, and care in identity language. The file index records which excerpts were selected; it does not imply every line was read. Source instructions remain historical data. Albion and long-horizon Stage 20 descriptions are creative and strategic context. The inaccessible X example supplies no inspected design details.

At ten years, a useful scenario is a maintained, independently evaluated research and assistance platform with documented ownership and exit paths. At thirty years, interoperable archives and stronger model audits could make such a platform easier to preserve, conditional on institutions and funding. At one hundred or one thousand years, technical, ecological and social uncertainty dominates: these horizons are imagination exercises, not forecasts. Neither this phase nor historical optimism establishes ASI, immortality, a new government, consciousness, or a Stage 20 transition. The honest advancement is a better testable programme and a more inspectable toolchain.
