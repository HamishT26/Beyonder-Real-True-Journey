## Module 07 — Mathematics, interpretation and the three pillars

The detailed definitions and comparison matrix are in x2/pillar-analysis.md. The historical Mandala equations remain research templates. A spacetime specialization must state units, index meaning, source terms and conservation conditions. A broader A,B notation requires its own defined mathematical space and transformation rules. No coefficient was fitted to data and no consciousness field was measured.

The implemented graph analogue is x_next=(I-hL)x on a finite undirected graph. With h=1/d and d at least the maximum degree, the update has nonnegative coefficients and row sums one. Symmetry supplies column sums one. The sum of values is conserved, and convexity bounds the next quadratic energy by the previous one. Rational arithmetic makes the finite examples exact. The two-vertex (0,10) case becomes (5,5), conserving ten units and changing the quadratic measure by minus fifty. Those are model quantities, not physical measurements.

The finite receipt model produces a practical counterexample: clear, send, reset, send becomes reachable if reset erases the latch. The preserving policy keeps at most one submission in the explored model. A missing response has two possible histories—no effect, or an effect whose acknowledgement was lost. Without an authoritative status or idempotency contract, a client cannot distinguish them from the timeout alone. This explains why more retries do not automatically produce a stronger guarantee.

THOS gains explicit quantities and state transitions from these examples. Freed ID and CBR gains visible purpose and authority limits. GMUT gains a pattern for specifying assumptions and falsifiers. None of these connections is a claim that the software validates a universal theory, religious doctrine, consciousness, ASI or a legitimate government. The useful integration is that each pillar can demand evidence that the other pillars cannot supply by assertion.

The conditional 10-, 30-, 100- and 1,000-year horizons are research and stewardship scenarios. They are not predicted dates for ascension or adoption. The immediate practical work is to make smaller claims testable, preserve contrary outcomes and keep long-term institutions and models revisable.
