The retained counterexample

For p(u) = (u − 1/2)², the degree-two Bernstein coefficients are 1/4, −1/4 and 1/4. Their hull is [−1/4, 1/4], even though the polynomial is never negative. Splitting at u = 1/2 gives a tighter [0, 1/4] enclosure. A negative control coefficient alone is insufficient to prove a negative function value.
